# Learn ChatGPT and AI tools in Tamil: free courses and where GEO fits

You can learn ChatGPT and other AI tools in Tamil for free. Entri has a free ChatGPT course in Tamil with 8 videos, a free prompt engineering course and a free AI tools master class. Paid options start with a 4-hour Udemy course. Once you can use these tools, GEO is the next step: how AI assistants pick which businesses to recommend.

We sell a GEO course, The GEO Blueprint, so we have a stake in the last section. The course details come from each provider's own page. Several come from search results, so we mark them "reported".

## Free courses in Tamil

**ChatGPT course.** Entri's [free ChatGPT course in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-coding-courses/chatgpt-course-in-tamil/) has 8 videos: 7 on ChatGPT essentials and 1 on Gemini. It covers setup, prompting, content writing and creative brainstorming. It needs basic computer skills, basic Tamil and a very basic grasp of English. The page doesn't state a length, and it mentions an Excel course, so it may contain a copy error.

**Prompt engineering.** Entri also lists a [free prompt engineering course in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-coding-courses/prompt-engineering-course-in-tamil/) (reported from search results, not opened).

**AI tools.** Entri's [AI and Cloud Tools master class in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-coding-courses/ai-and-cloud-tools-master-class-in-tamil/) covers ChatGPT, Grammarly, NoteGPT, Descript, QuillBot and Trello, using free versions (reported).

## Paid courses in Tamil

**Udemy.** A "GenAI Specialist in Tamil" course is about 4 hours 12 minutes long and covers ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Canva AI. Search results showed about 104 students and a 4.5 rating from 23 ratings, though another listing showed 4.3 from 10 ratings (reported). Check the page for the current price.

**Masai School.** Its Tamil programmes are taught in Tamil with IIT Patna certification. The AI and ML essentials track is 4 months at 10 hours a week (reported, prices not seen).

These teach AI tools generally. None of what we found teaches how to get a business recommended by an AI assistant.

## Where GEO fits

Learning ChatGPT teaches you to use an AI assistant. GEO is the other side: why an assistant recommends one business and not another. India has about [100 million weekly ChatGPT users](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/), OpenAI's second-largest market, according to a February 2026 report. Businesses that never appear in those answers have a gap, and GEO is the work of closing it.

You don't need to know GEO to use AI tools. But if you can already use them, you're ready for GEO. There's a [GEO course in Tamil from Entri](https://entri.app/explore/free-digital-marketing-courses/generative-engine-optimization-course-in-tamil/), and ours, mostly in Tamil. See [is there a GEO course in Tamil](/blog/geo-course-in-tamil).

## A sensible order

1. **Learn to use ChatGPT.** Watch the free Tamil course. Ask it 10 questions about your own work.
2. **Learn how it picks businesses.** Take a GEO course. Ours is ₹499 and about 1.5 hours.
3. **Practise on one real website.** Audit a small shop's page.
4. **Decide if you want more.** If you find it useful and want to dig deeper, a longer programme makes sense.

Follow the [4-week plan](/blog/how-to-learn-geo-in-tamil) for hours and tasks.

## Questions, answered

### Is there a free ChatGPT course in Tamil?
Yes. Entri's free ChatGPT course in Tamil has 8 videos and needs only basic Tamil and a very basic grasp of English. Its page doesn't state a length. Entri also lists free prompt engineering and AI tools courses in Tamil.

### Do I need to know English to learn AI tools?
A little. ChatGPT's menus are in English, and Entri's Tamil course says so. You can type your questions in Tamil. Many tool names and terms, such as prompt, stay in English even in Tamil lessons.

### What's the difference between learning ChatGPT and learning GEO?
ChatGPT courses teach you to use the tool. GEO teaches you how the tool chooses which businesses to recommend, so you can help a business get named. You can learn the first without the second, but the second builds on the first.

### Are Udemy Tamil AI courses worth it?
We can only report what search results showed: a 4-hour GenAI course with about 104 students and a 4.3 to 4.5 rating. Ratings vary by listing and the sample is small. Read recent reviews and check what the course covers first.

# How to learn GEO in Tamil: a 4-week plan for beginners

You can learn the basics of GEO in Tamil in 4 weeks with about 3 to 4 hours a week. Week 1: learn ChatGPT in Tamil. Week 2: take a GEO course in Tamil. Week 3: practise an audit on one real website. Week 4: learn to price and write a proposal. The cost can stay under ₹500. The hours are our suggestion, not a measured result.

We sell one of the courses in this plan, The GEO Blueprint. The rest are free or from other providers, linked below.

## Before you start

You need a phone or laptop and a basic grasp of English. Entri's Tamil ChatGPT course says so itself: it needs [basic Tamil and a very basic grasp of English](https://entri.app/explore/free-coding-courses/chatgpt-course-in-tamil/), because ChatGPT's menus are in English. Many GEO words, such as schema and AI Overviews, stay in English even in Tamil lessons.

## Week 1: get comfortable with ChatGPT in Tamil

Goal: ask an AI assistant good questions.

- Watch Entri's [free ChatGPT course in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-coding-courses/chatgpt-course-in-tamil/). It has 8 videos: 7 on ChatGPT basics and prompting, and 1 on Gemini. The page doesn't state a total length.
- Practise: ask ChatGPT and Gemini the same 3 questions about a local shop, for example "best bakery near me in Madurai", and note which businesses they name.

## Week 2: learn what GEO is

Goal: explain how AI assistants pick which businesses to recommend.

- Take Entri's [free GEO course in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-digital-marketing-courses/generative-engine-optimization-course-in-tamil/). Its page gives no length and has one inconsistent line, so watch the first lesson before you commit.
- Or take [The GEO Blueprint](/#curriculum): 6 modules, about 1.5 hours, mostly in Tamil with English terms mixed in, ₹499. It covers the 6 pillars of GEO and a live audit demo.

## Week 3: practise one audit

Goal: do the work once on a real site.

Pick a small business website, perhaps a friend's shop. Write down 5 questions a customer would ask an AI assistant. Ask them. Note whether the business appears, and what the pages say about hours, prices and location.

The research paper behind GEO tested 9 page changes. The best 3, [adding statistics, adding quotations and citing sources](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2311.09735), improved visibility by about 30 to 40% in the authors' own test. Use them as a checklist on the page you audit. It's a lab result, not a guarantee.

## Week 4: learn to price and write a proposal

Goal: turn the audit into an offer.

- Watch Part 4 of Rank Prompt's [free English course](https://www.rankprompt.com/geo-course/), which covers service tiers, pricing benchmarks and proposal templates. It's about 4 lessons of the 20.
- Or use module 5 of The GEO Blueprint, which covers 2 ways to charge for GEO work, plus its client questionnaire.
- Write a one-page offer for the business you audited. Don't promise rankings or citations.

## An illustrative example

This is an illustrative plan, not a real student. Someone in Madurai has a phone, a laptop and ₹499. In week 1 they watch 8 short ChatGPT videos on the phone. In week 2 they watch The GEO Blueprint on the laptop. In week 3 they audit a relative's textile shop. In week 4 they write a one-page offer. Total spend: ₹499. Total time: about 15 hours.

## Questions, answered

### How long does it take to learn GEO in Tamil?
A first pass takes about 4 weeks at 3 to 4 hours a week in our plan. That's a suggestion, not a measured figure. Watching the lessons takes under 3 hours. Practising on a real site is where most of the time goes.

### Do I need to know English to learn GEO?
A little. ChatGPT's menus are in English, and terms like schema stay in English. Entri's Tamil ChatGPT course says a very basic grasp of English is enough. You don't need fluent English to start.

### Can I learn GEO in Tamil for free?
Yes, partly. Entri's GEO course in Tamil and its ChatGPT course in Tamil are free. For selling, the best free lessons we found are in English, in Rank Prompt's course. The GEO Blueprint is ₹499.

### What should I do first?
Learn to use ChatGPT well, then learn how it picks businesses, then audit one site. In that order, each step is small. Skipping to selling before you've done one audit is the common mistake.

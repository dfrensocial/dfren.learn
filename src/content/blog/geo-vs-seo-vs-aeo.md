# GEO vs SEO vs AEO vs AI SEO: what is the difference?

SEO gets your page ranked in a list of search results. GEO (generative engine optimization) gets your business mentioned or cited inside an AI assistant's answer. AEO (answer engine optimization) aims to be the direct answer. AI SEO is the everyday name for SEO aimed at AI results. The 4 overlap heavily, and most of the work is shared.

Here are plain definitions, what's actually different, and which one to learn first.

## The 4 terms side by side

| Term | Plain meaning | Where it matters | Who uses the word |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO | Improving pages so they rank in a list of results | Google and Bing result pages | Everyone in marketing |
| GEO | Improving how often and how well AI answer tools mention or cite you | ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews | Researchers, practitioners, courses |
| AEO | Making your content easy to pull out as a direct answer | AI answers, featured answers, voice | Marketers, taught beside GEO |
| AI SEO | SEO work aimed at AI results | Both lists and AI answers | Searchers and clients in India |

"GEO" comes from a 2023 research paper by Pranjal Aggarwal and 5 co-authors. It reports that GEO methods [can boost visibility by up to 40% in generative engine responses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735). That's an upper bound from the authors' own tests, not a typical result.

Course makers treat GEO and AEO as one topic. Rank Prompt's free course, for example, opens with a lesson on [defining GEO and AEO](https://www.rankprompt.com/geo-course/) together.

## What is actually different from SEO?

The big change is where people read the answer. 3 numbers show it:

- **AI answers sit on top of Google.** Google's AI Overviews had [2 billion monthly users](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/23/googles-ai-overviews-have-2b-monthly-users-ai-mode-100m-in-the-us-and-india) in July 2025, according to Alphabet's CEO.
- **People click less when they appear.** In a [Pew Research study](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) of 900 US adults, users clicked a regular result in 8% of visits with an AI summary, against 15% without one. That's US data from March 2025.
- **Assistants have their own audience.** India has about [100 million weekly ChatGPT users](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/), OpenAI's second-largest market.

So the goal widens from "rank on page 1" to "be named in the answer". That's what GEO and AEO describe.

## What stays the same?

In our experience, the basics carry over: clear pages that crawlers can read, specific facts, named authors and trustworthy sources. We think that's why many SEO people say GEO isn't new. We cover that argument in [is GEO just rebranded SEO](/blog/is-geo-just-rebranded-seo-vs-2027).

## An illustrative example

Here's an illustrative example, not a real client. A 12-seat café in Besant Nagar.

- **SEO task:** rank for "cafe in Besant Nagar" in Google's list, with a good Google Business Profile and a menu page.
- **GEO task:** be named when someone asks ChatGPT "where can I work from a café near Besant Nagar with good wifi?". That depends on whether pages and reviews describe the wifi, hours and seating in plain words.
- **AEO task:** a clear answer block on the page, such as "The café has wifi, 12 seats and opens at 8 am", so an assistant can lift it.

One page edit can help all 3. That's why people treat them as one skill.

## Questions, answered

### What is the difference between GEO and SEO?
SEO aims to rank pages in a results list. GEO aims to get your business mentioned or cited inside an AI assistant's answer. They share most of the work: clear, specific, trustworthy pages. GEO adds checks on how AI tools read and quote you.

### Is AEO the same as GEO?
Close, not identical. AEO focuses on being the direct answer, including featured answers and voice. GEO covers being mentioned or cited by generative AI. Courses usually teach both together, and the same page edits help both.

### What is AI SEO?
AI SEO is the everyday name for SEO aimed at AI results. It's the label customers search for most in India. It covers the same ground as GEO and AEO, so a clear service page can mention all 3 terms.

### Should I learn GEO or SEO first?
Learn the shared basics first. SEO fundamentals help with every term. Then add the GEO-specific checks: how AI tools pick sources, how to be quotable and how to measure mentions. A free 90-minute course is enough to start.

# Does the language you ask in (Tamil, English) change which businesses AI recommends?

It can. In a 2026 test of 1,650 answers, the language of the question changed which brands an AI recommended, sometimes by a wide margin. We found no study that tests Tamil specifically, so we can't say how it would behave for your business. The safe step is to test your own questions in Tamil and in English and see who is named.

We sell a course taught mostly in Tamil, so we're interested in the answer. The evidence below is limited, and we say so.

## What did the test find?

Reza Moaiandin of SALT.agency wrote up the test in [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/lost-in-translation-how-language-changes-which-brand-ai-recommends/591585/) on 13 October 2026. He asked the same shopping and booking questions in five languages (English, Arabic, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Persian), 40 runs each, mostly on Claude with web search off. Some examples from his data:

| Question | What changed with language |
|---|---|
| Return flight, London to Tenerife | In Persian, British Airways led with 52.5%. In Russian, easyJet had 72.5% and British Airways had 0% |
| Hotel in Tenerife | Hotel Botánico took 32.5% of English answers and did not appear in Russian |
| Headphones under £400 | Sony led in English at 50%, and reached 87.5% to 100% in the other languages |
| Project management software | Monday.com led in every language |

So some categories shifted a lot and some didn't. With web search turned on, the winning brand changed in 7 of 10 category and language combinations. A smaller comparison found Gemini matched Claude's winners in 5 of 10 combinations and ChatGPT in 1.

## What are the limits?

The author states them: the sample is small, collected in one session on 7 September 2026, and covers some models, categories and languages. Many percentages may sit within the margin of error, and one mention in 40 isn't evidence. With web search on, 42.7% of responses hit the output limit, so those figures may be "soft". He says results may not hold from one week to the next. It isn't peer reviewed, and it doesn't include Tamil or any Indian language.

Other reports say the same thing, including a press-release study by FTA Global on city and language effects, and an arXiv preprint. We haven't verified those methods, so we don't rely on them.

## What does it mean for Tamil searches?

We can only reason by analogy, and we label it that way.

- **The answer for a Tamil question may differ from the English one.** The test shows this happens for other languages.
- **Which pages the assistant can read matters.** If your pages are only in English, a Tamil question may pull other sources.
- **Tamil support exists but varies.** ChatGPT [lists Tamil](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8357869-how-to-change-your-language-setting-in-chatgpt), the Gemini app [lists Tamil](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13575153), and Google's AI Mode [added Tamil in October 2025](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/supercharging-search-for-india-new-languages-in-ai-mode-search-live-debuts/). We found no official Tamil statement for Claude or Perplexity.
- **People mix languages.** Many Indians type Tamil words in English letters, or mix both. Test those spellings too.

## How do you test it for your business?

1. Write 10 customer questions in English and the same 10 in Tamil.
2. Add a third set in the mixed style your customers actually type.
3. Run each three times in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and log who is named. See the [setup guide](/blog/test-prompts-chatgpt-gemini-perplexity).
4. Compare appearance rates across languages.

If you appear in English and not in Tamil, consider adding Tamil text to key pages. That's our suggestion, not something a study has proven.

## Questions, answered

### Will AI recommend a different business if I ask in Tamil?
It might. A test in five other languages found the language of the question changed which brands were named in several categories. We found no Tamil test, so check your own questions.

### Should my website be in Tamil?
Use the language your customers search in. Adding Tamil to key pages is cheap to try, and you can measure whether it changes who is named.

### Does ChatGPT understand Tamil?
OpenAI lists Tamil as a supported language. We haven't found a published quality comparison for local business questions.

### Is one language better for being recommended?
We found no evidence either way. The test above shows language can change results in both directions.

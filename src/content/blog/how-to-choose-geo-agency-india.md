# How to choose a GEO agency in India: a buyer's checklist

Choose an agency that shows you a baseline before it starts, says what it will do and measure, explains that results can't be guaranteed and gives you data you can check yourself. Walk away from guaranteed mentions or fixed timelines. Published GEO retainers in India range widely, from ₹35,000 a month to several lakh, and those figures come from sellers, so compare scopes, not only prices.

We teach GEO and could be seen as competing with agencies, so read this with that in mind. We've kept to checkable points.

## Ten questions to ask

1. **What will you check first?** A good answer: what assistants say about us today, our crawler access and our key pages. Ask to see a sample baseline.
2. **How do you test AI answers?** Answers vary a lot. In one study ChatGPT and Google's AI returned the same brand list [less than 1% of the time](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/). The agency should run prompts several times and report appearance rate, not a single screenshot.
3. **What exactly will you deliver each month?** Pages, fixes, reports. Get it in writing.
4. **What do you promise?** The right answer is no guarantee. OpenAI says [placement in ChatGPT search is not guaranteed](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt).
5. **How will we measure progress?** Baseline, monthly appearance rate, Search Console and analytics data. You should have access to the accounts.
6. **How long before we see anything?** Expect "weeks to a few months", with a review point. Distrust "30 days guaranteed".
7. **Who writes the content, and how do you fact-check it?** Content should have real facts and sources.
8. **Do you use special files or tricks?** Google's guide says llms.txt and special files [neither harm nor help](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) in Google Search.
9. **Can you show past work with real dates?** Case studies with baselines, not only screenshots.
10. **What do you need from us?** Access, approvals, facts. If the answer is "nothing", be careful.

## Red flags

- Guaranteed mentions, rankings or traffic. India's [CCPA guidelines](https://ccpa.doca.gov.in/files/CCPA_Notification.pdf) require factual claims to be capable of substantiation, and the [ASCI Code](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code) requires truthful advertising. Neither names GEO, but a guarantee is hard to substantiate.
- A score from "our AI tool" with no method. Chatbots give different scores each run. See [why](/blog/why-chatgpt-gives-different-website-score).
- No baseline, only a report at the end.
- A long lock-in contract before any proof.
- Pressure and vague jargon.

## What do prices look like?

Published figures are from sellers:

- upGrowth Digital's [benchmark page](https://upgrowth.in/geo-aeo-pricing-benchmark-india-2026/) says mid-market GEO retainers in India run ₹75,000 to ₹3,50,000 a month, and one-time audits ₹1,50,000 to ₹8,00,000.
- A [Redefine ROI](https://www.theatdb.com/news/redefine-roi-launches-generative-engine-optimization-geo-service-to-help-brands-get-cited-by-chatgpt-google-ai) launch notice reports a service starting at ₹35,000 a month.

We found no independent survey of what Indian businesses actually pay. See [GEO retainer pricing in India](/blog/geo-retainer-pricing-india) and [what GEO work can be worth](/blog/geo-freelancer-earnings-india-2027).

## Can you do it yourself first?

For a small business, yes: complete your [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/in/business-profile/), fix key pages and run a monthly prompt check. See [GEO on a zero budget](/blog/geo-small-business-zero-budget). It also makes you a better buyer.

## Questions, answered

### How much does a GEO agency cost in India?
Published retainers run from about ₹35,000 to ₹3,50,000 a month, and above ₹5,00,000 for enterprise, per seller pages. Independent data is missing.

### Should a GEO agency guarantee results?
No. Placement in AI answers can't be guaranteed, and guarantees are hard to substantiate under advertising rules.

### What should a GEO report include?
A baseline, what changed, appearance rate over time, traffic and Search Console data, and next steps.

### Can my SEO agency do GEO?
Possibly. Google says optimizing for its AI features is still SEO. Ask the same ten questions.

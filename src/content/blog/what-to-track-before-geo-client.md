# What to track before you start a GEO client: an onboarding baseline checklist

Before you change anything on a client's site, find out what's already being tracked and record a starting point: Google Analytics, Search Console, Microsoft Clarity, a panel of AI answers and a snapshot of their listings and key pages. Without a baseline you'll have nothing to compare against later. Then plan to check again at about three months.

We teach this in the client onboarding module of our course, so we have a stake. The checklist below is our practice, and we link official docs for each tool.

## Step 1: Ask what's already connected

Ask the client whether they use Google Analytics, Google Search Console and Microsoft Clarity. If they do, you have history. If they don't, connect them first, before any GEO work starts. See our guides: [connect Google Analytics 4](/blog/connect-google-analytics-4-website), [connect Clarity](/blog/connect-microsoft-clarity-google-analytics) and [track who visits your site](/blog/track-who-visits-your-website).

## Step 2: The baseline checklist

| Item | What to record | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Traffic | Sessions and top sources, last 3 to 12 months | GA4 |
| AI referrals | Visits from AI assistants, if any | [GA4 setup](/blog/see-if-chatgpt-sends-visitors-to-your-site) |
| Search queries | Top queries, clicks and impressions | [Search Console](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7042828) |
| AI impressions | Impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode | Google's [Generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) |
| Bing and Copilot | Citations | Bing's [AI Performance report](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview) (public preview) |
| AI answers | Who is named for 10 to 20 customer questions, three runs each, dated | [Prompt panel](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business) |
| Crawler access | robots.txt and firewall status for AI bots | [OpenAI bots page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) |
| Key pages | Top 5 pages: first line, facts, FAQ, dates | [Audit steps](/blog/how-to-run-a-geo-audit) |
| Listings | Google Business Profile completeness, review count | [Google help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) |

## Step 3: Save evidence

Export reports as PDFs or CSVs with the date. Screenshot AI answers with the date, the assistant and the prompt. Keep everything in one shared folder. Remember that answers vary, so the panel is a sample. See [why answers differ](/blog/why-chatgpt-answers-differ-every-time).

## Step 4: Set expectations in writing

Tell the client on day one that content has to be published, crawled, indexed and then cited, which realistically takes weeks to a few months. Agree to a review at about three months. That's our practice, not a measured rule. See [how long GEO takes](/blog/how-long-does-geo-take).

## Step 5: Mind consent and privacy

Analytics and session recording tools set cookies and process visitor data. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, consent has to be free, specific, informed and unambiguous where personal data is processed. Make sure the client's consent setup is in place before you connect Clarity. This isn't legal advice.

## Step 6: Schedule the checks

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Baseline saved |
| Monthly | Re-run the prompt panel, log appearance rate |
| Month 3 | Full comparison against baseline |
| Quarterly | Refresh key pages and re-baseline |

## Questions, answered

### What should I track before starting GEO?
Traffic, search queries, AI referrals, a dated panel of AI answers, crawler access, key page state and listings.

### What if the client has no analytics?
Set it up first and note the start date. Your baseline begins then, and earlier history won't exist.

### How long should I wait before judging results?
We suggest reviewing at about three months. No source publishes a reliable timeline.

### Do I need Clarity?
It's optional. It shows how visitors behave on pages, and it needs a consent setup.

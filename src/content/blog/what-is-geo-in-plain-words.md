# What is generative engine optimization (GEO)? A plain-words guide for 2026

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of making your website and content easy for AI assistants, such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, to find, understand and recommend. SEO tries to get your page ranked in a list of links. GEO tries to get your business named inside the answer. The term comes from a [2023 research paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) by Indian and US researchers.

We teach GEO, so we like the topic. Below we keep to what the sources say, and we mark opinion as opinion.

## Where does the word come from?

The authors of the paper "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization" (arXiv 2311.09735, first posted in November 2023) used the phrase for methods that [boost a page's visibility in generative engine answers](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2311.09735). They tested 9 content changes on 10,000 queries. Adding sources, quotations and statistics did best in their test, and keyword stuffing did no better than doing nothing. See [what the paper really found](/blog/geo-paper-up-to-40-percent).

## Why does it matter now?

People are asking assistants instead of searching. India has about [100 million weekly ChatGPT users](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/), according to a February 2026 report. Google's AI Overviews had [2 billion monthly users](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/23/googles-ai-overviews-have-2b-monthly-users-ai-mode-100m-in-the-us-and-india) by July 2025. And in a Pew Research study of US users, people clicked a traditional result in [8% of visits when an AI summary appeared, against 15% when it didn't](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/).

That doesn't mean search is dead. Ahrefs' tracker found AI chatbots sent [0.28% of web traffic in March 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/). The change is real, and it's still small as a source of visits. The bigger shift is in what people read before they ever click.

## What does GEO work look like?

In practice it's ordinary website work, aimed at a different reader:

- **Let the crawlers in.** Assistants that search the web need to reach your pages.
- **Put the answer first.** Say what you do, for whom and where, in the first two lines.
- **Back claims with sources and numbers.**
- **Be mentioned elsewhere,** on reviews, directories and other sites.
- **Keep pages current** and cover the follow-up questions a buyer would ask.

Our course groups these into 6 pillars: technical optimization, answer-first content, citation authority, AI comprehension, content freshness and content depth.

## What GEO is not

- **Not a guarantee.** OpenAI says [placement in ChatGPT search is not guaranteed](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt), and AI answers change from run to run.
- **Not a special file.** Google's guide says you don't need [new machine readable files or llms.txt](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) to appear in Google Search. See [is llms.txt enough?](/blog/llms-txt-vs-geo-is-it-enough-2026)
- **Not entirely separate from SEO.** The same Google guide says optimizing for generative AI search is "still SEO" for Google's features. Other assistants may behave differently. We explain the overlap in [GEO vs SEO vs AEO](/blog/geo-vs-seo-vs-aeo) and [is GEO just rebranded SEO](/blog/is-geo-just-rebranded-seo-vs-2026).

## Questions, answered

### What does GEO stand for?
Generative engine optimization. A "generative engine" is an AI system that writes an answer, such as ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews, instead of only listing links.

### Is GEO the same as AEO?
Not quite. AEO, or answer engine optimization, is an older term covering direct-answer surfaces such as featured snippets and voice search. People often use the two interchangeably. See [what is AEO](/blog/what-is-aeo).

### Do I need GEO if my SEO is good?
Good SEO helps, and Google says its AI features follow the same basics. Studies disagree on how much AI citations overlap with top rankings, so check what assistants actually say about you. We cover that in [why Google rank doesn't equal AI mentions](/blog/google-rank-vs-chatgpt-mentions).

### Can a small business do GEO?
Yes, for the basics, and without paid tools. Complete your Google Business Profile, fix your key pages and check results monthly.

## How we compared

We used 4 rules:

- **Rupees only.** Dollar prices use ₹94.72 per US dollar ([x-rates](https://www.x-rates.com/calculator/?from=USD&to=INR&amount=1), 3 November 2026). Your card may add fees.
- **GST included where the page adds it.** IIM Skills lists 18% GST on top of its fee, which adds ₹6,282 to the Expert price.
- **One price per option.** For Coursera we used the yearly Coursera Plus price on the India page, ₹6,999 (about ₹583 a month, billed annually).
- **Hours only if the page states them.** Where it doesn't, we say so.

## 3 traps when you read a course price

**Dollar prices aren't the final price.** A $199 bundle is about ₹18,848 at today's rate. The rate moves daily, and your bank may add a fee.

**GST changes the number.** An Indian provider quoting ₹34,900 plus GST charges ₹41,182. Always add 18% when a page says "plus GST".

**A subscription isn't a course price.** Coursera Plus covers many courses. ₹6,999 is only good value if you finish the GEO specialisation inside the year. Its [rating is 3.3 out of 5 from 12 reviews](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/generative-engine-optimization-geo), a small sample.

## What price doesn't tell you

Price says little about quality. Rank Prompt's course is free and has 20 lessons. Only Coursera shows a public rating for a GEO course in our list.

Hours don't tell you much either. In our view, more hours don't mean more learning: if you can learn the GEO workflow in about 1.5 hours and run your first audit, why spend 10 or 24 hours first? Longer programmes suit people who want a certificate, mentors or the whole of digital marketing.

Guarantees are another thing price can't tell you. IIM Skills advertises a "100% Job Guarantee" and a 95% placement record, but its page doesn't say how it counted. India's consumer regulator banned false guarantee claims in coaching in November 2024, according to [Taxmann's analysis](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/analysis-consumer-protection-act-ccpa-targets-coaching-centres-promises/). Ask any provider for the terms in writing.

## Which price tier suits whom

Don't start at the top of the ladder. Spending ₹40,000 before you know GEO suits you is the costly way to find out. A sensible order:

1. **Start at ₹0 to ₹499.** A free course is a good sample, and The GEO Blueprint is the paid step we suggest. Together that's about 3 hours of teaching for ₹499 or less.
2. **Practise on one real site.** Run an audit and write one proposal.
3. **Go deeper only if it clicks.** If you want mentors, a certificate or an internship, that's when Coursera (₹6,999 a year), Trust Insights (about ₹9,377 to ₹18,848) or IIM Skills (₹41,182 and up) are worth thinking about.

## Questions, answered

### How much does a GEO course cost in India?
From ₹0 to ₹70,682 including GST. Free courses exist, Coursera is ₹6,999 a year, Trust Insights is about ₹9,377 to ₹18,848, and IIM Skills in Chennai is ₹41,182 to ₹70,682. All checked on 3 November 2026. Ours is ₹499, among the lowest-priced paid options we found, and it's the one we suggest starting with.

### Is a low-priced GEO course a poor one?
Not necessarily. Price doesn't predict quality here. A free 90-minute course has 20 lessons, and a ₹499 course is about 1.5 hours long. A higher price mostly buys more hours, a certificate, mentors or an internship. Check the page for what you'd actually get. We suggest starting with The GEO Blueprint because it's short enough to finish and built around a first audit.

### Does GST apply to GEO courses?
It does when the provider is registered and says so. IIM Skills adds 18% GST to its fees. The course is charged at ₹499 at checkout, with no extra tax added on top. Always check whether a page says "plus GST" before comparing totals.

### Are dollar courses cheaper than Indian ones?
Often not once you convert. Trust Insights' $99 course is about ₹9,377, and its $199 bundle is about ₹18,848. That's far above a ₹499 course, though the content and length differ. Compare hours and topics, not just price.

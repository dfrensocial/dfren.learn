# How to make money with AI in 2026: learn GEO, then sell it (an honest version)

One way to potentially make money with AI in 2026 is to offer a service: helping local businesses get named by AI assistants. That work is called GEO, and sellers publish prices from ₹35,000 a month. Whether it earns you anything depends on finding paying clients, and we found no independent data on what beginners earn. So treat every income promise as marketing, including anything from us. Here is what the service is, what it could be worth in simple arithmetic and a realistic first 90 days.

We sell a GEO course, so we want you to try this. We also can't promise you'll earn anything, and we don't. What we can show is how the service is priced and how to work out the numbers yourself.

## What people are searching for

Searches for making money with AI are steady in India, but searches for learning AI are far bigger. In a [Google Trends chart for India](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?q=make%20money%20with%20AI,AI%20course,ChatGPT%20course,digital%20marketing%20course%20in%20chennai,AI%20course%20in%20tamil&geo=IN&date=today%2012-m) over 12 months, "AI course" averaged 64.5 and "make money with AI" averaged 0.7. Trends scores are relative, from 0 to 100, not volumes.

In plain words, far more people want to learn AI than to earn from it directly. That's a sensible instinct. Skills first, income later.

## Why this work exists

Customers now ask AI assistants for recommendations. India has about [100 million weekly ChatGPT users](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/), OpenAI's second-largest market, according to a February 2026 report. Google's AI Overviews had [2 billion monthly users](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/23/googles-ai-overviews-have-2b-monthly-users-ai-mode-100m-in-the-us-and-india) in July 2025. A business that isn't named in those answers may never be seen.

GEO, or generative engine optimization, is the work of checking whether an assistant names a business and improving the pages so it's more likely to. See [what GEO is](/blog/geo-vs-seo-vs-aeo).

- An Indian agency publishes [retainers of ₹75,000 to ₹3,50,000 a month](https://upgrowth.in/geo-aeo-pricing-benchmark-india-2026/) for mid-market work.
- Small-business SEO retainers run about ₹15,000 to ₹60,000 a month, [according to an agency page](https://seo.irpr.agency/pricing/small-business-seo/) (reported).

These are asking prices from sellers. They aren't what beginners earn. Dollar prices use ₹94.72 per US dollar ([x-rates](https://www.x-rates.com/calculator/?from=USD&to=INR&amount=1), 3 November 2026). See [GEO earnings in India](/blog/geo-earnings-india) for the full data and its limits.

## What it could be worth: the maths, with your own numbers

Income from a service is price times clients, minus costs. Suppose you set a one-off audit price of ₹15,000 and sold 2 in a month: that's ₹30,000 of revenue before tax, tools and your own hours. Sell none and it's ₹0. These are made-up inputs for the arithmetic, not results anyone achieved and not typical results. Put your own price in, then test the one number nobody can supply: whether a real business will pay. The full model is in [what GEO work can be worth in India](/blog/geo-earnings-india).

## A realistic first 90 days

This is our suggestion, not a forecast, and not a promise of clients.

**Days 1 to 30: learn.** Follow the [30-day plan](/blog/learn-geo-30-days). A free course plus The GEO Blueprint takes about 3 hours of video. Practise on one site.

**Days 31 to 60: audit real businesses.** With permission, audit 2 or 3 local businesses. Some people offer the first one free to build a sample. That's your choice, not a rule. Write up what you checked and what you'd change.

**Days 61 to 90: make offers.** Contact 10 businesses with a short, specific note based on what you found. Quote a one-off audit before a retainer. See [how much to charge](/blog/how-much-geo-audit).

What happens after that, we can't tell you. You may win clients, or none, and nobody has measured the odds.

## What can go wrong

- **Overpromising.** Nobody can guarantee an AI assistant will name a business. Say what you'll check and change, not what will happen.
- **Unstable answers.** AI answers change between runs, so record the date of every check.
- **Competition.** Agencies already sell GEO, and some free courses teach it.
- **Slow sales.** Finding clients takes work that no course replaces.
- **Fast change.** Advice that works now may not in a year. Re-check.

## Questions, answered

### Can I make money with AI in India in 2026?
Some people may, by selling services around AI tools. GEO audits and content retainers are one example, and sellers publish prices for them. We found no independent data on typical earnings, so we can't say you will. Learn the skill, try it on a real business and judge from your own results.

### Is GEO a good side hustle?
It might suit you if you like research and writing and can find local clients. Demand for the term is still small in India, and no source measures what side-hustlers earn. Start with one audit before you invest more.

### How long does it take to earn from GEO?
We don't know. No source measures it. Our plan has you learn in 30 days and make offers by day 90, but a first client may take longer. Don't plan your finances around it.

### Do I need a degree or a certificate?
We found nothing that requires one. Free and cheap courses teach the basics, and a real audit shows more than a certificate. A bigger programme with a certificate is optional, and worth considering only later.

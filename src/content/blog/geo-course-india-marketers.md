# AI SEO and GEO courses in India: what to look for before you pay

Check 7 things before you pay for an AI SEO or GEO course in India: the syllabus, the hours, the full price with GST, the language, the refund terms, proof of quality and any job or income promises. Most people start with a broad digital marketing course, then add GEO. Checked 3 November 2026.

We sell a ₹599 including GST course, The GEO Blueprint, so we have a stake in this. We use the same checklist on our own course below. In our view it's a sensible first step for most beginners, and the reasons are at the end. That is our opinion, so check the facts yourself.

## What people search for before they find GEO

Most learners in India start with a general course. In a [Google Trends chart for India](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?q=SEO%20course,digital%20marketing%20course,AI%20SEO%20course,AEO%20course,GEO%20course&geo=IN&date=today%2012-m) over 12 months, "digital marketing course" averaged 81.2, "SEO course" averaged 10.9 and "AI SEO course" averaged 0.8. Trends scores are relative, from 0 to 100, not volumes.

So a good AI SEO or GEO course is often an add-on to something broader. The checklist works for both cases.

## The 7 checks

### 1. Does the syllabus cover the whole job?
A useful benchmark is Rank Prompt's free course, which covers [4 areas in 20 lessons](https://www.rankprompt.com/geo-course/): the basics, fixing your own site, getting AI tools to cite you, and selling the work. If a paid course covers less, ask why.

### 2. Does it state the hours?
A page that says "28 lessons" or "6 months" without hours is hard to compare. [Coursera's GEO specialisation](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/generative-engine-optimization-geo) states 9, 8 and 7 hours for its 3 courses. [Trust Insights](https://academy.trustinsights.ai/bundles/geo-power-pack) states lessons but not hours.

### 3. What is the full price, with GST?
Indian providers often list a fee "plus GST". [IIM Skills](https://iimskills.com/digital-marketing-course-in-chennai/) lists ₹34,900 plus 18% GST, which is ₹41,182. Add 18% before you compare.

### 4. Which language is it taught in?
Most GEO courses we found are in English. Entri has a [free Tamil course](https://entri.app/explore/free-digital-marketing-courses/generative-engine-optimization-course-in-tamil/). 98% of Indian internet users access content in Indic languages, according to the [IAMAI-Kantar 2024 report](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/01/16/dcm51-biz-iamai-internet-usage-report.html). Learn in the language you think in.

### 5. What are the refund terms?
Ask before paying. Read them in writing, not on a sales call. Ours: no refunds for a change of mind, only an extra charge or access we can't fix within 30 days ([refund policy](/refund-policy)).

### 6. Is there proof of quality?
A public rating helps, even a small one. Coursera's GEO specialisation shows 3.3 out of 5 from 12 reviews. Many Indian course pages show testimonials without a source. Ours has no student reviews yet and lists no certificate.

### 7. Does it promise jobs or income?
Be careful. IIM Skills advertises a "100% Job Guarantee" and a 95% placement record, and its page doesn't explain how it counted. India's consumer regulator issued guidelines in November 2024 that bar false guarantee claims in coaching, [according to Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/analysis-consumer-protection-act-ccpa-targets-coaching-centres-promises/). That analysis is secondary, so read the official text. Ask what a "guarantee" means in practice and what you get if it fails.

## Questions, answered

### How do I choose an AI SEO course in India?
Check the syllabus, the stated hours, the full price with GST, the language, the refund terms, any public rating, and whether it promises jobs. If a page can't answer these, ask before you pay. A free course shows you what a good syllabus looks like. After that, we suggest The GEO Blueprint at ₹599 including GST as a low-risk first paid step.

### Is a digital marketing course enough to learn GEO?
Only if it has a GEO module, and few do. IIM Skills' Chennai programme lists GEO as module 7. Many broader courses mention AI but may not teach GEO. Check the syllabus for GEO or AEO by name.

### Are job guarantees in course ads reliable?
Treat them as advertising until you see the terms. A page that says "100% job guarantee" should say what counts as a job, who decides and what happens if you aren't placed. Ask in writing before paying.

### How long does it take to learn GEO?
A first pass takes about 90 minutes with a free course, about 1.5 hours with The GEO Blueprint, or about 24 hours with Coursera's specialisation, suggested over 8 weeks. Getting good at audits takes practice on real websites beyond any course.

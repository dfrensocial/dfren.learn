# How to start a GEO agency or freelance practice without a tools budget

You can start a GEO practice with free tools: Google Analytics, Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, a free audit tool or two and a spreadsheet for prompt checks. Spend your first weeks learning the method and auditing one real site. We found no data on how many new GEO practices earn anything, so treat this as a low-cost way to test the idea, not a promise.

We sell a course that covers this workflow, so we have a stake. Everything below is free except where noted.

## What to do in your first 30 days

1. **Learn the method.** A short course, plus Google's two AI pages. See [the 30-day plan](/blog/learn-geo-30-days).
2. **Practise on your own site or a friend's.** Set up tracking, take a baseline and fix three pages.
3. **Do one real audit** with permission. Follow [the audit steps](/blog/how-to-run-a-geo-audit).
4. **Write a sample report** you can show.

## How do you find first clients?

Start with people who know you: existing SEO or web clients, local businesses you already deal with. Offer a small, fixed-price baseline, not a big retainer. Show the sample report. No course can promise a client, and we don't. See [how to sell GEO to SEO clients](/blog/sell-geo-to-seo-clients).

## What to package

Three offers cover most of it: an audit, a fix pass and a monthly retainer. See [GEO service packages](/blog/geo-service-packages-sample-tiers) and [how much to charge](/blog/how-much-geo-audit).

## What to avoid

- **Guarantees.** India's [CCPA guidelines](https://ccpa.doca.gov.in/files/CCPA_Notification.pdf) require claims about facts to be capable of substantiation, and the [ASCI Code](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code) requires truthful claims. See [a proposal that doesn't promise rankings](/blog/geo-proposal-without-promising-rankings).
- **Copying a competitor's case study.** Show only your own, with real dates.
- **Skipping the baseline.** Without it, you can't show progress.
- **Buying tools too early.** Add a paid tracker only when you have enough prompts or clients to justify it. See [tools by budget](/blog/ai-visibility-tools-by-budget).

## Business basics to check

Business registration, GST and contracts depend on your situation, and we aren't qualified to advise on them. Check with an accountant. Use a written agreement for every client.

## Questions, answered

### Can I start a GEO agency with no budget?
You can start learning and auditing at no tool cost. Whether it becomes a business depends on finding clients, which we can't promise.

### What tools do I need first?
GA4, Search Console and a spreadsheet. Add a free audit tool and Bing Webmaster Tools.

### Do I need a team?
Not at the start. A solo freelancer can run the first audits.

### How many clients do I need?
No source tells you. Work from your own costs and rates. See [what GEO work can be worth](/blog/geo-freelancer-earnings-india-2027).

# GEO checklist 2026: what to fix first on your website

Fix these 12 things in order: let crawlers in, make key pages indexable, put the answer in the first two lines, add facts and sources, add a short FAQ, keep your business details identical everywhere, complete your Google Business Profile, get listed and reviewed, show dates and refresh pages, add structured data if it matches the page, set up tracking, then re-check. Items 1 to 3 matter most, because nothing else works if bots can't read your pages.

We make an audit tool and teach a course on this, so we have a stake. We've tied each item to a source or marked it as our practice.

## The checklist

**1. Check that crawlers can reach you (source: OpenAI, Google).** Open yourdomain.com/robots.txt. OpenAI says [OAI-SearchBot](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) surfaces sites in ChatGPT search, and to be eligible you should allow it and confirm your host or CDN allows OpenAI's IP addresses. A robots.txt change can take about 24 hours to take effect.

**2. Make sure key pages are indexed (Google).** Google says a page must be [indexed and eligible to show in Search with a snippet](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) to appear in AI Overviews. Search site:yourdomain.com and check your main pages appear.

**3. Put the answer in the first two lines (our practice).** Say what you do, for whom, where and the price range. This is good practice, not a published rule.

**4. Add facts, numbers and sources (research).** The [GEO paper](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2311.09735) found adding sources, quotations and statistics lifted visibility in its test, and keyword stuffing didn't.

**5. Add a short FAQ with real questions (our practice).** Write the questions customers actually ask, and answer each in two or three sentences.

**6. Keep your name, address and phone identical everywhere (our practice).** Ambiguity confuses people and machines alike.

**7. Complete your Google Business Profile (Google).** Google India says [creating one is free](https://business.google.com/in/business-profile/), and complete profiles are [more likely to show up in local results](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).

**8. Get listed and reviewed (correlation).** In an [SE Ranking study](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/new-data-top-factors-influencing-chatgpt-citations/561954/), sites on several review platforms averaged more ChatGPT citations than those that weren't. It's a correlation across large sites.

**9. Show dates and refresh key pages every quarter (correlation).** In the same study, pages updated within three months averaged more citations than outdated ones.

**10. Add structured data only if it matches the page (Google).** Google's guide says it isn't required for generative AI search. See [schema markup for AI answers](/blog/schema-markup-for-ai-answers).

**11. Set up tracking before you change anything.** Connect [Google Analytics](/blog/connect-google-analytics-4-website) and Search Console, and record a baseline. Google's [Generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) show impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode.

**12. Re-check monthly.** Ask the same 10 customer questions in the main assistants and log who is named. Our [free method](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business) shows how.

## What you can skip

- **llms.txt.** Google's guide says Google Search ignores it. See [is llms.txt enough?](/blog/llms-txt-vs-geo-is-it-enough-2026)
- **Rewriting every page.** Start with your top three.
- **Keyword stuffing for AI.** The paper found it did no better than doing nothing.

| Week | Items | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1, 2, 11 | 2 to 3 hours |

The hours are our estimates for a small site.

## Questions, answered

### What should I fix first for GEO?
Make sure crawlers can reach your pages and that your key pages are indexed. Then put a direct answer in the first two lines of each important page.

### How long does the checklist take?
For a small site, a few weeks part-time by our estimate. Results can take weeks to months to show, and no one publishes a reliable timeline.

### Do I need a developer?
For robots.txt, structured data and speed fixes, often yes. Writing answer-first copy, FAQs and facts doesn't need one.

# GEO retainer pricing in India: what agencies publish, in rupees

Published GEO retainers in India run from about ₹35,000 a month at the low end to ₹3,50,000 for mid-market work, and above ₹5,00,000 for enterprise. Sources disagree on the floor, none explains how it collected its numbers, and every one sells GEO. Treat them as asking prices, not what clients pay.

We sell a course, not retainers, so we have no price to defend here. All numbers come from the pages linked, checked on 3 November 2026.

## Why the numbers disagree

upGrowth, a Pune agency, calls ₹75,000 to ₹3,50,000 the [mid-market range](https://upgrowth.in/geo-aeo-pricing-benchmark-india-2026/) and says a ₹1,50,000 retainer over 6 months, a ₹9,00,000 commitment, is the floor for "a real Indian GEO engagement". Redefine ROI starts at ₹35,000. Other pages in our search put starter tiers anywhere from ₹50,000 to ₹2,50,000 (reported from search snippets, not opened).

The gaps come from 3 things: how much work is included, who's selling, and what a "retainer" means. An agency quoting ₹35,000 may deliver an audit and a report. One quoting ₹3,50,000 may include PR placements and research.

upGrowth's page doesn't say how it gathered its India ranges. Its client results, such as Claude-sourced traffic growing 16 times between December 2025 and March 2026, are self-reported.

## How a small business compares

For context, an agency page says most small-business SEO retainers in India run [₹15,000 to ₹60,000 a month](https://seo.irpr.agency/pricing/small-business-seo/), with most owners settling around ₹20,000 to ₹40,000 (reported, agency marketing). A GEO retainer at ₹75,000 or more is well above that. That's why many small shops start with a one-off audit instead. See [how much to charge for a GEO audit](/blog/how-much-geo-audit).

## 6 things to check in a GEO quote

1. **What's delivered each month,** in numbers: articles, pages fixed, platforms tracked.
2. **How results are measured.** AI answers change between runs, so ask how they'll handle that.
3. **The contract length.** A 6-month minimum is common in the sources.
4. **Who does the work.** A named strategist or a rotating team.
5. **Whether results are promised.** Be wary of any guarantee of citations or rankings.
6. **What's extra.** PR, tools and reporting may cost more.

## Questions, answered

### How much does GEO cost per month in India?
Published retainers run from about ₹35,000 to ₹3,50,000 for most work, and above ₹5,00,000 for enterprise. The sources disagree and all are sellers. upGrowth, the most detailed, puts mid-market work at ₹75,000 to ₹3,50,000.

### Is a ₹35,000 GEO retainer realistic?
One agency lists it as a starting price. We couldn't see what it includes. Another source calls ₹1,50,000 a month the floor for a real engagement. Ask what you get each month before you decide.

### Do GEO retainers include guaranteed results?
They shouldn't. Nobody can guarantee that an AI assistant will cite a business. upGrowth describes performance-linked deals with a per-citation bonus, which is different from a guarantee. Be wary of any promise.

### Is a retainer or an audit better for a small business?
For most small shops, an audit first. It costs less than several months of retainer and shows what to fix. Retainers make sense once there's ongoing content and tracking work. That's our view, not a sourced finding.

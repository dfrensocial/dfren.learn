# GEO service packages: audit, content and retainer, with sample tiers

Most GEO work fits three packages: a one-off audit, a fix pass that implements the audit's findings, and a monthly retainer for content and tracking. The tiers below are a sample structure for you to price from your own hours, not market rates. Published benchmarks exist, and they come from sellers, so use them as reference points only.

We teach two of these packages in our course, so we have a stake. Prices below are examples built from a formula, not promises of what you can charge.

## The three packages

| Package | What's included | Billing |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Audit | Baseline of AI answers, crawler and page checks, off-site mentions, written findings and a prioritised fix list | One-off |
| 2. Fix pass | The "Now" and "Next" fixes from the audit: access, answer-first rewrites on key pages, FAQs, consistent details | One-off, scoped from the audit |
| 3. Retainer | Monthly content, refreshes, prompt-panel re-checks and a report | Monthly |

This mirrors the two ways our course teaches: charge to fix what an audit finds, and charge on an ongoing basis for content. Each package should say what you don't promise. See [a proposal that doesn't promise rankings](/blog/geo-proposal-without-promising-rankings).

## Sample tiers

Illustrative, so you can see the shape. Replace the hours with your own.

| Tier | Scope | Your hours (example) | Your rate (example) | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter audit | Up to 10 pages, 10 prompts, findings note | 8 | ₹1,000 | ₹8,000 |
| Standard audit | Up to 30 pages, 20 prompts, listings review, fix list | 20 | ₹1,000 | ₹20,000 |
| Fix pass | Implement top fixes on 5 key pages | 15 | ₹1,000 | ₹15,000 |
| Monthly retainer | 2 pages or posts, refreshes, monthly re-check and report | 12 a month | ₹1,000 | ₹12,000 a month |

The formula is hours times your rate, plus a margin for the unpaid time you spend finding and managing clients. The ₹1,000 rate is an example we chose, not a market figure. See [how much to charge for a GEO audit](/blog/how-much-geo-audit) for a worked example.

## What do sellers publish for comparison?

- **Audit pricing (US).** Alejandro Rioja's [GEO audit pricing guide](https://alejandrorioja.com/geo-audit-pricing-what-to-charge-clients/) (22 August 2026) lists about $500 to $750 for a single site under about 50 pages and $750 to $1,500 for larger or multi-location ones. That's roughly ₹47,358 to ₹71,037 and ₹71,037 to ₹1,42,073 at ₹94.72 a dollar ([x-rates](https://www.x-rates.com/calculator/?from=USD&to=INR&amount=1)). The author also sells consulting.
- **Retainers (India).** upGrowth Digital's [benchmark page](https://upgrowth.in/geo-aeo-pricing-benchmark-india-2026/) says mid-market GEO retainers run ₹75,000 to ₹3,50,000 a month and one-time audits ₹1,50,000 to ₹8,00,000. It's an agency's marketing benchmark with no stated method.

These are asking prices from sellers aimed at larger clients. A small local business may expect far less, and you can't assume you can charge what they charge. See [GEO retainer pricing in India](/blog/geo-retainer-pricing-india).

## How do you scope it?

1. **Count pages and prompts.** More pages and more questions mean more hours.
2. **List deliverables, not outcomes.** "Rewrite the top 5 pages answer-first" is a deliverable. "Be recommended by ChatGPT" is an outcome you can't deliver.
3. **Set a review point,** such as three months, to compare against the baseline.
4. **Put client duties in writing:** access, approvals and facts.
5. **Limit revisions** so scope doesn't grow without a new quote.

## Questions, answered

### What packages should a GEO freelancer offer?
Start with an audit, then a fix pass, then a retainer for content and tracking. Offer the audit first as a one-off so clients can try you with low risk.

### How do I price a GEO retainer?
Estimate monthly hours, multiply by your rate and add a margin for client management. Compare with published figures, knowing they come from sellers.

### Should the audit be free?
Some sellers do a free first look. That's your choice, not a rule. A free check can cost you hours with no client at the end, so keep it short and defined.

### Can I price GEO higher than SEO?
We found no evidence on what clients accept. One agency says GEO sits above SEO retainers, but that's its own claim. Price from your scope and your hours.

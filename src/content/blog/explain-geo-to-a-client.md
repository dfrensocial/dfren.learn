# How to explain GEO to a client who has never heard of it

Explain it in one sentence: "Customers now ask AI assistants like ChatGPT who to buy from, and GEO is making your business easy for those assistants to find and describe correctly." Then show them what the assistants say about their business today. A real example does more than any definition. Keep the numbers few and sourced, and say plainly that nobody can guarantee a mention.

We teach this in our course, so we like it when clients understand it. The numbers below come from public sources, with limits.

## The one-sentence version

"Search is changing. Customers ask ChatGPT or Google's AI who to choose and get a short answer. GEO means making sure that answer is accurate and includes you."

Avoid jargon such as "generative engines", "citations" and "entities" in the first minute. Use "AI answers" and "mentions".

## Show, don't tell

Before the meeting, run 5 customer questions about the client's category in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, three times each. Show a simple table: who was named, who wasn't. If the client is missing, that's your opening. If they appear, say so, and talk about keeping it. Our [free method](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business) shows how, and the caveat: answers vary, so present it as a sample.

## Three numbers you can use

1. India has about [100 million weekly ChatGPT users](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/) (a February 2026 report).
2. Google's AI Overviews had [2 billion monthly users](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/23/googles-ai-overviews-have-2b-monthly-users-ai-mode-100m-in-the-us-and-india) by July 2025.
3. In a US Pew study, people clicked a result in [8% of visits with an AI summary against 15% without](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appeared-in-the-results/).

Be honest about scale: AI chatbots sent [0.28% of web traffic](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/) in Ahrefs' March 2026 tracker. The shift is in what customers read, more than in clicks, so far.

## What clients ask, and honest answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Is this just SEO?" | Much of it overlaps. Google says optimizing for its AI features is [still SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide). GEO adds checking what assistants say and building mentions elsewhere |
| "Can you get us to number 1 in ChatGPT?" | No one can guarantee that. OpenAI says [placement isn't guaranteed](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt). We improve your odds and measure |
| "How long will it take?" | We expect weeks to a few months and will review at about three months. See [how long GEO takes](/blog/how-long-does-geo-take) |
| "Do we need a new website?" | Usually not. We fix and add to key pages |
| "What will it cost?" | Start with a small fixed-price baseline. See [packages](/blog/geo-service-packages-sample-tiers) |
| "How will we know it worked?" | Baseline first, then monthly appearance rate, plus traffic data |

## What not to say

- "AI will replace Google." There's no such evidence.
- "We'll make ChatGPT recommend you."
- Any income, ranking or timeline guarantee. India's [CCPA guidelines](https://ccpa.doca.gov.in/files/CCPA_Notification.pdf) require factual claims to be capable of substantiation.

## A one-page handout

1. What changed (three numbers).
2. What we found for you (the table).
3. What we'd fix first (three items).
4. How we'll measure (monthly, against baseline).
5. What we can't promise.

## Questions, answered

### What is GEO in simple words?
Making your business easy for AI assistants to find, understand and recommend correctly.

### How do I convince a sceptical client?
Show their own baseline and the public numbers, and don't overclaim. A small, low-cost first step helps.

### Should I use the word "GEO"?
Use it once, then say "AI answers". Many clients know "SEO" better. See [GEO vs SEO vs AEO](/blog/geo-vs-seo-vs-aeo).

### What if the client's competitors already appear?
Show which, and what their pages state that the client's don't, such as prices or areas served.

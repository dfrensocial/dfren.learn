# The GEO Blueprint by dfrenLearn: what's inside, who it's for and what it costs

The GEO Blueprint is a short practical course on generative engine optimization (GEO) from dfrenLearn, run by DFREN Marketing Agency in Chennai. It has 6 modules and about 1.5 hours of video, costs ₹499 plus 18% GST (₹588.82 in total) one time and is taught mostly in Tamil with English terms mixed in. It covers how AI assistants pick answers, the 6 pillars of GEO, a live audit and two ways to charge for GEO work. It has no certificate and no student reviews yet.

We make this course, so this page is our own description. For an outside comparison with other courses, read [where to learn GEO](/blog/where-can-i-learn-geo-in-india-2027).

## Who made it?

The course is taught by K Sai Anirudh, Founder of AiVirex Innovations, a technology studio. It's sold on dfrenLearn, which is operated by DFREN Marketing Agency, a partnership firm in Tambaram, Chennai. The team also makes Litmus, a GEO audit tool at [litmus.aivirex.in](https://litmus.aivirex.in), which the course uses in a live demo. See [About dfrenLearn](/about) for the full details.

## What are the 6 modules?

| # | Module | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How AI picks its next word | A plain-language look at how a model produces an answer, with no equations |
| 2 | SEO vs GEO | What changed, where the two overlap and where they differ |
| 3 | The 6 pillars of GEO | Technical optimization, answer-first content, citation authority, AI comprehension, content freshness and content depth, each with a quick win |
| 4 | Why manual audits don't work | Why asking a chatbot to grade your site changes every run, then a live audit with Litmus |
| 5 | Two ways to turn GEO into income | Charging to fix what an audit finds, and charging monthly for content |
| 6 | Client onboarding and proof | Setting up tracking, setting expectations and turning a before-and-after into a case study |

You also get a client research questionnaire, a Claude Code prompt pack and a quick-win checklist.

## Who is it for?

- **Business owners** who want their own site to show up in AI answers.
- **Marketers, freelancers and agencies** who want to offer GEO audits or content to clients.
- **Tamil speakers** who want to learn in the language they think in, with English terms where the industry uses them.

You don't need SEO experience first.

## Who is it not for?

- People who want a **certificate**. There isn't one.
- People who want **mentoring or live classes**. It's recorded video.
- People looking for a **long programme**. It's deliberately short. For a longer option, see [free vs paid GEO courses](/blog/free-vs-paid-geo-course).
- Anyone hoping for **guaranteed results**. Nobody can guarantee that an AI assistant will name a business, and the course says so.

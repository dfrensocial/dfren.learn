# Are free GEO courses enough, or is a paid one worth it?

For the basics, free GEO courses are enough. Rank Prompt's free course has 20 lessons and about 90 minutes of teaching, including how to price GEO work and write proposals. If you want something the free ones don't give you, such as lessons mostly in Tamil, ready-made client files or a single path to follow, our pick is The GEO Blueprint at ₹599 including GST. Checked 3 November 2026.

We sell The GEO Blueprint, so that pick is our opinion. We've tried to be fair to the free options, because they're good.

## What the free courses actually teach

Rank Prompt's [free course](https://www.rankprompt.com/geo-course/) is organised in 4 parts:

1. **Introduction (5 lessons):** defining GEO and AEO, how AI search changes buyer discovery, SEO vs GEO, and 7 AI platforms.
2. **Internal GEO and AEO (6 lessons):** auditing a site, structuring content so AI can quote it, schema markup, and a technical checklist.
3. **External GEO (5 lessons):** how AI platforms choose brands to cite, building a citation network, digital PR and monitoring.
4. **Selling and delivering GEO (4 lessons):** a live audit, service tiers, pricing benchmarks, proposal and pitch deck templates.

The page says it's "100% free, no card needed" and takes about 90 minutes. That's a full path from basics to a proposal.

Joe Davies' [30-minute mini-course](https://joedavies.beehiiv.com/p/build-a-5m-geo-agency) adds an agency view: how to position yourself, package GEO as a retainer and what deliverables matter. Entri offers a [free course in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-digital-marketing-courses/generative-engine-optimization-course-in-tamil/) on GEO vs SEO, AI Overviews, schema and AEO.

## What a free course may not give you

Be careful here. We only list what we could verify.

- **A different language.** Rank Prompt and Joe Davies are in English. Entri is in Tamil, but its page is inconsistent and doesn't state a length.
- **Files you can use on day one.** The free pages mention templates for proposals. We couldn't check whether they include a client research questionnaire or prompts for writing content.
- **A single method.** Free courses usually lead somewhere, such as a tool, a service or a bigger programme. That's not a flaw, but notice where each one is leading you. Ours is too: it leads to this paid course.

## When paying makes sense

Pay if one of these is true for you:

- You learn faster in Tamil than in English alone.
- You want a client questionnaire and a prompt pack as ready-made files, not just lessons.
- You prefer 1 short course with one method over 4 separate sources.
- You'd rather pay ₹599 including GST once than spend a weekend comparing options.

Don't pay if you read English comfortably, enjoy self-study and want to see whether GEO suits you. Start with Rank Prompt.

## A fair test: do the free one first

Spend 90 minutes on Rank Prompt's course. Then ask yourself 3 questions:

1. Can I explain how an AI assistant picks which business to recommend?
2. Can I run a basic audit on a real site?
3. Do I know what to charge and how to write a proposal?

If you answered yes three times, you don't need a paid course. If you got stuck on a lesson because of the language, or you want the client files, that's when ₹599 including GST is worth considering. Read our [refund policy](/refund-policy) first: there are no refunds for a change of mind.

## Questions, answered

### Are free GEO courses good?
Rank Prompt's is detailed: 20 lessons covering basics, site fixes, citations and selling. Joe Davies' is short and agency-focused. Neither is built for Indian prices or languages. They're a good starting point and enough for many learners.

### Should I pay for a GEO course?
Pay if you want something specific the free ones don't offer, such as another language, ready-made client files or a single guided path. If you only want the basics, don't pay. Try a free course first and see what's missing. If you do pay, we suggest starting with The GEO Blueprint at ₹599 including GST, among the lowest-priced paid options we found, rather than a long programme.

### What is the best free GEO course?
Of the ones we found, Rank Prompt's. It has 20 lessons, takes about 90 minutes and covers selling too. For a free course in Tamil, Entri's is listed, but test a lesson first because its page contains a conflicting language line. If you'd rather have one short path mostly in Tamil with client files, we suggest starting with The GEO Blueprint at ₹599 including GST.

### Will a free course help me get clients?
It can teach you how to audit and price, but no course finds clients for you. Rank Prompt's part 4 covers proposals and pricing. Getting the first client still means reaching out to real businesses.

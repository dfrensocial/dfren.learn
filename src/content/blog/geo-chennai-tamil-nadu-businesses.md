# GEO for Chennai and Tamil Nadu businesses: what to do and what the data says

If you run a business in Chennai, Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Madurai or elsewhere in Tamil Nadu, the plan is the same as anywhere: make your pages easy for AI assistants to find and quote, say clearly where you are, and get mentioned on other sites. Tamil Nadu has a lot of businesses to compete with. A government policy note counted over 25 lakh Udyam-registered MSMEs in the state by May 2024. We found no study of how AI assistants recommend local businesses in Tamil Nadu.

We sell a GEO course in Tamil and English, so we have a view here. We've marked what is data and what is our opinion.

## How big the local picture is

- **MSMEs.** Tamil Nadu's own policy note says the state had [25,07,462 registered MSMEs](https://tn.gov.in/rti/msme/msme_e_pn_2024_25.pdf) on the Udyam portal as of 31 May 2024. Its district chart shows Chennai at 2.91 lakh, Coimbatore at 2.03 lakh, Salem at 1.33 lakh, Tiruppur at 1.30 lakh and Madurai at 1.10 lakh. A February 2026 [IBEF infographic](https://www.ibef.org/uploads/industry/Infrographics/large/MSME-Infographic-February-2026.pdf) puts Tamil Nadu third among states by share of Udyam registrations, at 7.95%.
- **Online.** TRAI's report released on 12 October 2026 counts [68.60 million internet subscriptions](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/QPIR_12102026.pdf) in Tamil Nadu at the end of June 2026. That counts connections, not unique people.
- **Language.** Nationally, 98% of internet users access content in Indic languages, according to the [IAMAI-Kantar 2024 report](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/01/16/dcm51-biz-iamai-internet-usage-report.html), and Tamil is among the most popular. We found no Tamil Nadu-only figure.
- **Industry.** The Government of India's [Invest India](https://www.investindia.gov.in/state/tamil-nadu) page names Coimbatore and Tiruppur as growth centres and says the state leads in exports of ready-made garments, cotton yarn, handloom and leather goods.

What this means: many similar businesses are competing in the same cities. Being easy to understand, with clear facts, is one way to stand out.

"Supported" doesn't mean equally good. Test your own questions in both languages and see who gets named.

## A short plan for a Chennai or Tamil Nadu business

This is our suggestion, built from the evidence in our other guides.

1. **Name your place precisely.** OpenAI says ChatGPT uses approximate location and suggests asking with a [city, neighbourhood or postal code](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt). Put "T. Nagar, Chennai" or "Peelamedu, Coimbatore" on your pages, not just "near you".
2. **Complete your Google Business Profile.** Google India says [creating one is free](https://business.google.com/in/business-profile/), and complete profiles are [more likely to show up in local results](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).
3. **Write answer-first pages.** Say what you do, where, for whom and at what price range in the first two lines.
4. **Cover both languages where it makes sense.** If your customers search in Tamil, have Tamil text on key pages. That's our advice. We found no study showing which language wins.
5. **Add proof that belongs to you.** Years in business, trade memberships, export markets, certifications. Google asks for [unique, experienced content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), and real local detail is hard for others to copy.
6. **Check and repeat.** Run the same 10 questions in Tamil and English every month. Our [free method](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business) shows how.

## An illustrative example

This is a made-up case to show the idea, not a real client. A garment maker in Tiruppur has a website that says "quality garments at best price". A buyer asks an assistant for "knitwear manufacturers in Tiruppur that export". The page gives the assistant nothing to quote. The maker rewrites the top of the page: what they make, minimum order, export markets, certifications they hold and the year they started, all things they can prove. The new text is easier to quote than the old one. Whether it gets named is not guaranteed.

## Questions, answered

### Is GEO useful for a local shop in Chennai?
Probably useful, but we have no local proof. We found no study on AI recommendations for small businesses in Tamil Nadu. A complete Google Business Profile and clear pages are low-cost starting points whatever the AI does.

### Should my website be in Tamil?
Use the language your customers search in. ChatGPT and Gemini list Tamil as supported. If you serve both, offer both, and test which version assistants quote.

### Is Chennai different from other cities for GEO?
The method is the same. What differs is competition and detail. Chennai had 2.91 lakh registered MSMEs in the state's 2024 chart, so being specific about your area and trade matters.

### Can a manufacturer in Coimbatore or Tiruppur use GEO?
Yes. Buyers ask assistants about suppliers too. State your products, capacity, certifications and markets plainly, and get listed on trade directories. We found no figures on supplier searches in AI tools.

## What are the risks of learning from videos?

- **Mixed quality and age.** AI search changes fast. A video from last year can mislead. Google's own guide, added in May 2026, says you [don't need llms.txt or special files](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), which older tutorials may contradict.
- **Hype.** Some videos promise results. OpenAI says [placement isn't guaranteed](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt).
- **Gaps in selling.** Many teach site fixes and skip proposals and pricing.
- **Time.** Piecing together a plan from many videos takes hours. More hours don't mean more learning.

## What are the risks of a course?

- You pay before you've watched it all. Check the refund terms: ours has none for change of mind, only for an extra charge or access we can't fix, as in the [refund policy](/refund-policy).
- A course can go stale too. Ask how updates work.
- It can't replace practice on a real site.

## How to choose

1. Watch one free video and one course preview. Pick the one you follow more easily.
2. If you want a client workflow, check the course covers selling, not only fixes.
3. Decide how you'll practise. Pick one real site.
4. Don't wait for a perfect course. Start, then add.

Our suggestion: use YouTube to sample the topic for an hour, then start small with a structured course, put it to work on one real site and go deeper later if you want.

# GEO vs SEO in 2027: is GEO just rebranded SEO? An honest answer

Mostly, yes. The basics of GEO are the basics of good SEO: clear pages, specific facts, trustworthy sources. What's genuinely new is where people read answers. AI summaries now sit above Google results, assistants have their own large audiences, and success is measured in mentions, not only clicks. No one has shown that GEO tricks give a typical, repeatable gain.

We teach GEO and sell a course on it, so we're not neutral. We've tried to give the sceptics a fair hearing and mark what's proven.

## What the sceptics say

The critique is simple: GEO is SEO with a new name. 2 examples:

- Dejan (dejan.ai) argues that [GEO is a rebrand of SEO](https://dejan.ai/blog/geo-to-seo/) (reported from a search snippet; we haven't read the full post).
- A 2026 BrightonSEO talk is titled ["Your ranking socks: 95% of GEO is recycled SEO"](https://brightonseo.com/events/san-diego-2026/talks/your-ranking-socks-95-of-geo-is-recycled-seo-heres-the-5-that-makes-it-rocks). We've only seen the title and summary, so the 95% is the speaker's claim.

We agree with the core of it. In our experience, a page that's clear, specific and trustworthy tends to do well in both places.

## What's new, in 3 changes

### 1. More answers are read without a click
In a [Pew Research study](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) of 900 US adults, users clicked a regular result in 8% of visits when a Google AI summary appeared, and 15% when it didn't. 18% of searches in the study produced a summary. That's US data from March 2025, and Google has disputed the method.

### 2. Assistants have their own large audiences
Google's AI Overviews had [2 billion monthly users](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/23/googles-ai-overviews-have-2b-monthly-users-ai-mode-100m-in-the-us-and-india) in July 2025. India has about [100 million weekly ChatGPT users](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/), OpenAI's second-largest market. Customers ask these tools for recommendations and may never open a results page.

### 3. Success looks different
With SEO you track rank and clicks. With GEO you also check whether an assistant names your business and cites your page. Those checks use different tools, and answers can change between runs.

## What nobody has proven

The original research paper says GEO methods [can boost visibility by up to 40%](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) in generative engine responses. That's an upper bound from the authors' own tests, not a typical gain. In our search, we found no independent study that repeats it on real businesses.

Gartner forecast in February 2024 that [traditional search volume would fall 25% by 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-02-19-gartner-predicts-search-engine-volume-will-drop-25-percent-by-2026-due-to-ai-chatbots-and-other-virtual-agents) as chatbots take share. That's a forecast, and the full report is paywalled. We found nothing confirming it happened.

## How to decide if it's worth your time

Ask 3 questions:

1. **Do your customers ask AI assistants for recommendations?** In India, many do. Ask 5 customers.
2. **Does an assistant name your business today?** Ask ChatGPT or Perplexity 3 questions a customer would ask. Note who gets named.
3. **Are your basics solid?** If your site is slow, thin or unclear, fix that first. It helps SEO and GEO alike.

If the answer to 1 is yes and to 2 is no, a few hours of GEO work is reasonable. That's a small bet, not a promise.

## Questions, answered

### Is GEO just SEO with a new name?
Mostly. The core work, clear and specific pages from trustworthy sources, is the same. What's new is the setting: AI summaries above search results, large assistant audiences and a need to check mentions, not just rank. It's SEO with a wider target.

### Does GEO actually work?
We can't say it works for sure. The original paper reports gains of up to 40% in its own tests. We found no independent study showing typical results on real businesses. The sensible view is a small, cheap test, not a big bet.

### Should I pay a GEO agency?
Check what they promise. Anyone who guarantees citations or rankings is overreaching. Ask what they'll measure, how often, and how they'd tell GEO gains apart from normal SEO gains. Many basics you can test yourself first.

### Is SEO dead because of AI?
We found no evidence of that. Gartner forecast a 25% fall in traditional search volume by 2026, but we found nothing confirming it. Pew found fewer clicks when an AI summary appears, in US data from March 2025. Search is changing, not over.

# Where can I learn GEO for ₹500? What each price gets you

₹499 buys The GEO Blueprint: about 1.5 hours of video, or ₹333 an hour. You can also learn GEO for ₹0 with a free 90-minute course. Paying makes sense if you want lessons mostly in Tamil, a ready-made client questionnaire and prompt pack, or one clear path instead of piecing it together yourself. Prices checked 3 November 2026.

We sell The GEO Blueprint, so we're one of the options here. Where we compare, we use facts from each course's own page.

## What does ₹0 get you?

More than you'd expect. Rank Prompt's [free GEO and AEO course](https://www.rankprompt.com/geo-course/) has 20 lessons and runs about 90 minutes. Its last part covers service tiers, pricing benchmarks and proposal templates, so selling is covered too. It says no card is needed.

Joe Davies' [30-minute mini-course](https://joedavies.beehiiv.com/p/build-a-5m-geo-agency) covers packaging GEO as a retainer. Entri has a [free course in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-digital-marketing-courses/generative-engine-optimization-course-in-tamil/), though its page doesn't give a length.

For many learners, that's enough. Try one before you pay for anything.

## What does ₹499 get you?

The [GEO Blueprint](/#curriculum) has 6 modules and about 1.5 hours of video:

1. How AI picks the next word
2. SEO vs GEO and what changed
3. The 6 pillars of GEO
4. Why manual audits don't work, with a live audit demo
5. Two ways to turn GEO into revenue
6. Client onboarding and proving it worked

You also get a client research questionnaire, a Claude Code prompt pack and a quick-win checklist. It's a one-time payment.

The course page doesn't list a certificate, live classes or job help, so don't buy expecting them. It also has no student reviews yet.

## A ₹500 plan for one weekend

This is an illustrative plan, not a promise of results.

1. **Saturday morning, 90 minutes:** watch Rank Prompt's free course. Note every term you don't know.
2. **Saturday afternoon, 1 hour:** pick one small business website, maybe a friend's shop, and write down 5 questions a customer would ask an AI assistant about it.
3. **Sunday, 1.5 hours:** watch The GEO Blueprint. Compare how it explains the 6 pillars with what you noted.
4. **Sunday evening, 1 hour:** use the client questionnaire to list what you'd ask that business owner.

Total spend: ₹499. Total time: about 5 hours, including practice.

## Questions, answered

### Is ₹500 enough to learn GEO?
Yes, for the basics. A free 90-minute course plus a ₹499 course gives you about 3 hours of structured teaching. Learning to do the work on a real website takes practice beyond that, and no course shortens it.

### Can I learn GEO completely free?
Yes. Rank Prompt's course is free and has 20 lessons. Joe Davies' mini-course is free, and so is Entri's Tamil course. You may need to adapt the examples to Indian businesses yourself.

### Do I need paid tools to practise GEO?
Not to start. Free GEO audit tools exist, such as [Juma's](https://geo.juma.ai/), which appeared in our search results. We haven't tested it. You can also ask ChatGPT or Perplexity your customer's questions and note which businesses they name.

### Can I learn GEO in Tamil for ₹500?
Yes. The GEO Blueprint is ₹499 and taught mostly in Tamil, with English terms mixed in. Entri also has a free GEO course in Tamil. We compare the Tamil options in [is there a GEO course in Tamil](/blog/geo-course-in-tamil).

### Will a ₹499 course help me get clients?
It can teach you what to check and how to package the work, but it can't find clients for you. No course can promise that, and ours doesn't. Treat the questionnaire and audit method as tools for the work.

# How to get cited in Google AI Overviews: what Google says and what studies show

Google says there is no special trick. To be linked in an AI Overview, a page must be [indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), and "there are no additional requirements" to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. No extra markup, no AI text file. What Google recommends instead is helpful, original content, a page that works well and content its crawler can reach.

We teach GEO, so we'd like there to be more levers. There aren't many that Google confirms, and we've kept to those.

## What Google says to do

Google's [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) was added in May 2026. Together with its [May 2025 post on succeeding in AI search](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2025/05/succeeding-in-ai-search), the advice comes down to this:

1. **Write non-commodity content.** Google defines it as unique expert or experienced takes that go beyond common knowledge. A page that repeats what ten others say gives the system no reason to pick it.
2. **Let Google reach your pages.** Don't block Googlebot, and make sure pages load with a normal status and can be indexed.
3. **Provide a good page experience.**
4. **Set snippet controls on purpose.** Tags like nosnippet and max-snippet limit how your content is featured. More restrictive settings mean less visibility in AI features.
5. **Keep structured data accurate.** If you use it, it must match what's visible. The guide says it isn't required for AI search, and there is no special schema.org markup to add.
6. **Keep your Business Profile and product data current** if they apply to you.

## What you don't need

The same guide says you don't need new machine readable files, AI text files or Markdown to appear in Google Search. Adding them [neither harms nor helps your visibility](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), because Google Search ignores them. That includes llms.txt. We cover that in a separate article. It also says there's no requirement to break your content into tiny pieces and no ideal page length.

## Do you have to rank in the top 10?

Studies disagree, and all come from tool sellers.

- [Ahrefs](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overview-citations-from-top-ranking-pages-drop-sharply/568637/) found 76% of AI Overview citations came from Google's top 10 in July 2025. A March 2026 update with a changed method found about 38%.
- [BrightEdge](https://www.brightedge.com/resources/weekly-ai-search-insights/rank-overlap-after-16-months-of-aio) reported only 16.7% of citations came from top 10 results, with no sample size given.
- [Surfer](https://surfer.ai/blog/ai-overviews-citation-sources) looked at 10,000 keywords and found 67.82% of cited pages weren't in the top 10 for the main query or for the related searches it generated.

Our reading: ranking helps and it isn't required. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode may use [query fan-out](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), which means several related searches across subtopics. A page that answers one subtopic well can be picked even if it doesn't rank first for the main phrase. See [Google rank versus AI mentions](/blog/google-rank-vs-chatgpt-mentions).

## Are AI Overviews available in Tamil?

Be careful here. Google launched AI Overviews in India in [English and Hindi on 15 August 2024](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/explore-communicate/expanding-the-helpfulness-of-ai-overviews/). In October 2024, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/03/google-flexes-its-edge-in-india-in-ai-showdown) that Google said it would add Bengali, Marathi, Telugu and Tamil in the coming weeks. Separately, Google's India blog says AI Mode [added Tamil and six other Indian languages](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/supercharging-search-for-india-new-languages-in-ai-mode-search-live-debuts/) in October 2025. We found no current official language list for AI Overviews, so test your own Tamil searches before assuming.

## How to see whether it's working

Google counts clicks from AI Overviews and AI Mode in the normal Performance report in Search Console, under the Web search type, as its [Search Console help page](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7042828) explains. In June 2026 Google also announced [Generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports), which show impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode by page, country and date. At launch they showed impressions only, with no clicks or position, and Google's note says they reached all websites by 31 August 2026. Open Search Console and look for them under your site's performance reports.

Also do a manual check: search your key questions in Google and note when an Overview appears and who is linked. [Here is a free method](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business).

## Questions, answered

### Do I need schema markup to appear in AI Overviews?
No. Google's guide says structured data isn't required for generative AI search and that there's no special schema.org markup to add. Structured data is still worth using for rich results if it matches your visible content.

### Can I opt out of AI Overviews?
Google documents controls such as nosnippet, data-nosnippet, max-snippet and noindex. They limit how your content appears in Search features, including AI. They also limit your regular snippets, so use them carefully.

### How long until my page is cited?
We found no official timeline. Google says a page needs to be indexed first. Beyond that, nobody has published a reliable figure, so track for weeks and months.

### Is paying for Google Ads a way into AI Overviews?
We haven't studied ads in AI features, and the guidance we reviewed is about organic eligibility. Don't assume paid placement buys a citation.

# Where can I learn GEO in India? Every option compared (2026)

You can also learn the basics free in about 90 minutes, or pay ₹41,182 and up for a long programme. We checked every price on 3 November 2026.

We sell The GEO Blueprint, so that pick is our opinion. The facts behind it come from each course's own page, and we say where we couldn't check.

## Which free GEO courses are worth your time?

Rank Prompt's free course is the most complete free one we found. It has [20 lessons in 4 parts, about 90 minutes in total](https://www.rankprompt.com/geo-course/): the basics, fixing your own site, getting AI tools to cite you, and selling GEO as a service. The page says "100% free, no card needed".

Joe Davies offers a [free 30-minute mini-course](https://joedavies.beehiiv.com/p/build-a-5m-geo-agency), dated 10 July 2025. It's aimed at agencies and covers how to package GEO as a retainer and which deliverables matter.

Entri has a [free GEO course in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-digital-marketing-courses/generative-engine-optimization-course-in-tamil/). Its page gives no length or lesson count, and one line says the course is taught in Malayalam. Watch the first lesson before you rely on it.

If you read English comfortably, free is a real way to sample the topic. The free options teach ideas well. What they leave you to work out is the next step: a repeatable audit, a client questionnaire and a price you can quote. That is the gap a short paid course can fill.

## What do the paid options add, and what do they cost you?

**Coursera.** The Edureka [GEO specialisation](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/generative-engine-optimization-geo) has 3 courses of 9, 8 and 7 hours. The page suggests 8 weeks at 5 hours a week. It's rated 3.3 out of 5 from 12 reviews, which is a small sample. Access comes with Coursera Plus at ₹6,999 a year, so you are paying for a year to study about 24 hours of English content. We found no selling lessons listed on the page.

**Trust Insights.** [GEO 101 and GEO 201](https://academy.trustinsights.ai/bundles/geo-power-pack) cost $99 and $149, and the bundle is $199 for 28 lessons. They're written for marketers. The page doesn't state hours or any selling lessons.

**IIM Skills.** Its Chennai [digital marketing programme](https://iimskills.com/digital-marketing-course-in-chennai/) has GEO as module 7 of 22, so GEO is one slice of a wider course. Expert is ₹34,900 plus GST (4 months live, 1 month virtual internship). Master is ₹59,900 plus GST (6 months and 2). It suits people who want the whole of digital marketing, an internship and a certificate. The page advertises a "100% Job Guarantee" and a 95% placement record but doesn't say how it counted them. Read the terms before you pay.

**The GEO Blueprint.** Everything is about GEO, with nothing else to sit through. 6 modules and about 1.5 hours of video cover how AI picks answers, the 6 pillars, a live audit with a fixed-rubric tool and two ways to charge for the work. It comes with a client questionnaire, a prompt pack and a quick-win checklist. It costs ₹499 once and is taught mostly in Tamil, with English terms mixed in. We think that is the fastest route from "what is GEO?" to your first audit and offer.

## How to choose in 4 questions

1. **What's your budget?** At ₹0, sample Rank Prompt for 30 to 90 minutes. At ₹499, take The GEO Blueprint and get the whole workflow in one evening. Spend more only after you know GEO is for you.
2. **How much time do you have?** The free courses take 30 to 90 minutes. The GEO Blueprint takes about 1.5 hours. Coursera is about 24 hours. IIM Skills runs for months. More hours don't automatically mean more learning. In our view, if you can learn the GEO workflow in 1.5 hours and put it to work on a real site, there's little reason to sit through 10 or 24 hours first.
3. **Will you sell GEO to clients?** Rank Prompt (4 lessons), Joe Davies and our module 5 cover it. Ours adds a client questionnaire and prompt pack to work with. We found no selling lessons on the Coursera or Trust Insights pages.
4. **Which language do you learn fastest in?** Tamil: Entri for a free course, ours for a structured one with client files. English: the rest.

## Questions, answered

### Is there a free GEO course in India?
Yes. Rank Prompt's 20-lesson course and Joe Davies' 30-minute mini-course are free, and both are in English. Entri offers a free course in Tamil. None of the three is built around Indian prices or clients, so you'll need to adapt the examples yourself.

### How long does it take to learn the basics of GEO?
A first pass takes about 90 minutes with Rank Prompt's free course. A structured course like Coursera's is about 24 hours of content, spread over roughly 8 weeks at 5 hours a week. Practice on a real site takes longer than watching.

### Do I need to know SEO first?
The courses we checked don't require it. Entri's page says no advertising experience is needed. Coursera's page says a basic grasp of digital marketing or SEO helps. If you've never heard of SEO, learn the idea first, then start.

### Can I learn GEO in Tamil?
Yes. Entri has a free GEO course in Tamil, though its page is inconsistent, so test one lesson first. The GEO Blueprint is taught mostly in Tamil, with English terms mixed in. Most other courses we found are in English only.

### How much does a GEO course cost in India?
From ₹0 to ₹70,682. The high end is IIM Skills' Master programme at ₹59,900 plus 18% GST. In the middle sit Coursera at ₹6,999 a year and Trust Insights at about ₹9,377 to ₹18,848. Ours is ₹499, among the lowest-priced paid options we found. Prices checked 3 November 2026.

# Where can I learn GEO? Every option, from free to ₹70,000

## Which free GEO courses are worth your time?

Rank Prompt's free course is the most complete free one we found. It has [20 lessons in 4 parts, about 90 minutes in total](https://www.rankprompt.com/geo-course/): the basics, fixing your own site, getting AI tools to cite you, and selling GEO as a service. The page says "100% free, no card needed".

Joe Davies offers a [free 30-minute mini-course](https://joedavies.beehiiv.com/p/build-a-5m-geo-agency), dated 10 July 2025. It's aimed at agencies and covers how to package GEO as a retainer and which deliverables matter.

Entri has a [free GEO course in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-digital-marketing-courses/generative-engine-optimization-course-in-tamil/). Its page gives no length or lesson count, and one line says the course is taught in Malayalam. Watch the first lesson before you rely on it.

If you read English comfortably, free is a real option. Start there.

## What do the paid options add?

**Coursera.** The Edureka [GEO specialisation](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/generative-engine-optimization-geo) has 3 courses of 9, 8 and 7 hours. The page suggests 8 weeks at 5 hours a week. It's rated 3.3 out of 5 from 12 reviews, which is a small sample. Access comes with Coursera Plus at ₹6,999 a year.

**Trust Insights.** [GEO 101 and GEO 201](https://academy.trustinsights.ai/bundles/geo-power-pack) cost $99 and $149, and the bundle is $199 for 28 lessons. They're written for marketers. The page doesn't state hours.

**IIM Skills.** Its Chennai [digital marketing programme](https://iimskills.com/digital-marketing-course-in-chennai/) has GEO as module 7 of 22. Expert is ₹34,900 plus GST (4 months live, 1 month virtual internship). Master is ₹59,900 plus GST (6 months and 2). The page advertises a "100% Job Guarantee" and a 95% placement record but doesn't say how it counted them. Read the terms before you pay.

**The GEO Blueprint.** 6 modules, about 1.5 hours of video, a client questionnaire and a prompt pack. It costs ₹499 once and is taught in a mix of Tamil and English.

1. **What's your budget?** At ₹0, start with Rank Prompt. Under ₹500, add The GEO Blueprint.

## Questions, answered

### Is there a free GEO course in India?
Yes. Rank Prompt's 20-lesson course and Joe Davies' 30-minute mini-course are free, and both are in English. Entri offers a free course in Tamil. None of the three is built for Indian prices or clients, so you'll need to adapt the examples yourself.

### How long does it take to learn the basics of GEO?
A first pass takes about 90 minutes with Rank Prompt's free course. A structured course like Coursera's is about 24 hours of content, spread over roughly 8 weeks at 5 hours a week. Practice on a real site takes longer than watching.

### Do I need to know SEO first?
The courses we checked don't require it. Entri's page says no advertising experience is needed. Coursera's page says a basic grasp of digital marketing or SEO helps. If you've never heard of SEO, learn the idea first, then start.

### Can I learn GEO in Tamil?
Yes. Entri has a free GEO course in Tamil, though its page is inconsistent, so test one lesson first. The GEO Blueprint is taught in a mix of Tamil and English. Most other courses we found are in English only.

### How much does a GEO course cost in India?
From ₹0 to ₹70,682. The high end is IIM Skills' Master programme at ₹59,900 plus 18% GST. In the middle sit Coursera at ₹6,999 a year and Trust Insights at about ₹9,377 to ₹18,848. Ours is ₹499. Prices checked 3 November 2026.

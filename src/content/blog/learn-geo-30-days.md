# How to learn GEO in 30 days: a beginner plan for India

You can learn the basics of GEO in 30 days with about 4 hours a week. Week 1: learn the ideas. Week 2: study the 6 pillars and the research. Week 3: audit one real website. Week 4: price your work and write a proposal. The plan costs ₹499 with The GEO Blueprint, or ₹0 if you stick to free lessons. The weekly hours are our suggestion, not a measured result.

We sell one of the courses used in this plan, The GEO Blueprint, and we built the plan around it: weeks 2 to 4 lean on it. That is our opinion. We also mark where free options work as well.

## Before you start

Pick one small business website to practise on, such as a friend's shop or your own. You'll use it in weeks 3 and 4. You also need access to ChatGPT or Perplexity, both of which have free versions.

## Week 1: learn the ideas (about 2 hours)

Goal: explain GEO in 2 sentences.

- Watch Part 1 of Rank Prompt's [free GEO course](https://www.rankprompt.com/geo-course/). Its 5 intro lessons define GEO and AEO, compare SEO and GEO, and survey 7 AI platforms.
- Prefer Tamil? Use Entri's [free GEO course in Tamil](https://entri.app/explore/free-digital-marketing-courses/generative-engine-optimization-course-in-tamil/) and check its first lesson.
- Ask ChatGPT 3 questions a customer might ask about your practice business. Write down which businesses it names.

## Week 2: study the 6 pillars and the research (about 4 hours)

Goal: know what to check on a page.

- Take [The GEO Blueprint](/#curriculum): 6 modules, about 1.5 hours, ₹499, mostly in Tamil with English terms mixed in. This is the spine of the plan. It covers how AI picks answers, the 6 pillars and a live audit demo with a fixed-rubric tool.
- Read the [original GEO research paper](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2311.09735). It tested 9 page changes on 10,000 queries. The best 3 were citing sources, adding quotations and adding statistics, which lifted visibility by about 30 to 40% in the authors' own test. Keyword stuffing did no better than doing nothing.
- Make a checklist of the pillars and the 3 changes.

## Week 3: audit one real website (about 5 hours)

Goal: produce one audit document.

1. Write 5 questions a customer would ask an AI assistant about the business.
2. Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Note who is named.
3. Check the business's page against your checklist: does it state facts with numbers, name its sources and answer questions directly? Follow the audit demo from The GEO Blueprint as your template.
4. Write a one-page summary of what's missing and what to fix first. The course's quick-win checklist helps you sort the fixes.

Don't promise results. Say what you checked and what you'd change.

## Week 4: price your work and write a proposal (about 3 hours)

Goal: a one-page offer.

- Use module 5 of The GEO Blueprint and its client questionnaire. For a second view, watch Part 4 of Rank Prompt's course: service tiers, pricing benchmarks and proposal templates.
- Read what others charge. One free course lists [audits from $500](https://www.rankprompt.com/geo-course/), about ₹47,358. Those are Western agency prices, so adjust for your market. See [how much to charge for a GEO audit](/blog/how-much-geo-audit).
- Write the offer for the business you audited.

## What you'll have after 30 days

One audit, one proposal and a checklist. No certificate and no client. A first client still means asking real businesses, and no course promises one.

## Tips that save time

- **Do one audit, not three.** Depth on one site teaches more than a skim of many.
- **Keep the questions.** Re-ask them in a month and compare. AI answers change between runs, so note the date.
- **Don't count hours as progress.** In our view, more hours don't mean more learning. If you can learn the GEO workflow in about 1.5 hours and run your first audit, why spend 10 or 24 hours first? Longer programmes suit people who want a certificate, mentors or the whole of digital marketing.
- **Don't buy tools yet.** Free versions of ChatGPT and Perplexity are enough to start.

## Questions, answered

### Can I learn GEO in 30 days?
You can learn the basics and do one audit. That takes about 14 hours in our plan. Getting good takes practice across many sites. Thirty days gets you started, not expert.

### How many hours a day do I need?
About 30 minutes a day, or 4 hours a week. Week 3 takes the most time, because you work on a real site. The lessons themselves take under 3 hours in total.

### Do I need to pay to learn GEO?
No. Rank Prompt's course is free and has 20 lessons, including pricing. Paying ₹499 for a short course in Tamil is optional. You'd pay more only for mentors or a certificate. We suggest the ₹499 Blueprint as week 2 anyway, because it's short and ends with an audit demo and 2 ways to charge.

### What should I practise on?
A small business site you can look at freely, such as a friend's shop or your own page. Ask AI assistants questions about it, compare the page with your checklist and write down gaps. Don't change a client's site without permission.

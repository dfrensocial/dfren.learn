# An AEO blueprint: a 7-step plan to get your content used as the answer

Answer engine optimization (AEO) means structuring your content so search and AI systems can use it as a direct answer. A workable blueprint has 7 steps: pick the real questions, answer first, structure each page, let the crawlers in, build mentions elsewhere, track results over time and refresh. Google says optimizing for its AI features is still SEO, so most of this is good page-making with a clearer focus on questions.

We teach GEO, which overlaps heavily with AEO. There's no single official AEO standard, so this is our practical plan, and we mark which steps have a source.

## Step 1: Pick the real questions (source: Google, our practice)

List 10 to 20 questions customers ask, in their words. Use sales calls, support emails and Google's own suggestions. Google says its AI features may use [query fan-out](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), meaning several related searches across subtopics, so cover the follow-up questions too.

## Step 2: Answer first (our practice)

Put the direct answer in the first two lines of the page and under each question heading. Then explain. This is common AEO practice. We found no platform statement that a certain length is required, so keep it as short as the answer allows.

## Step 3: Structure the page (Google, our practice)

- Use clear headings, with real questions as headings where natural.
- Add a short FAQ with direct answers.
- Use a table for comparisons and numbers for steps.
- Add structured data only if it matches the visible page. Google's guide says it [isn't required for generative AI search](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide). See [schema markup for AI answers](/blog/schema-markup-for-ai-answers).

## Step 4: Let the crawlers in (Google, OpenAI)

A page must be [indexed and eligible to show with a snippet](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) for Google's AI features. For ChatGPT search, OpenAI says to allow [OAI-SearchBot and its IP addresses](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt). Check robots.txt and your firewall.

## Step 5: Build mentions elsewhere (correlation)

In an [SE Ranking study](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/new-data-top-factors-influencing-chatgpt-citations/561954/), sites listed on several review platforms averaged more ChatGPT citations. It's a correlation. Complete your [Google Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091), get listed in the directories your buyers use and ask happy customers for honest reviews.

## Step 6: Track over time (SparkToro, Google)

Answers vary: in one study ChatGPT and Google's AI returned the [same brand list less than 1% of the time](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/). So track how often you appear across repeated runs, plus Search Console's [Generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports). Our [free method](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business) is a quick win, not a reliable tracker.

## Step 7: Refresh (correlation)

In the same SE Ranking data, pages updated within three months averaged more citations than outdated ones. Update your top pages each quarter with new facts, and show the date.

## A one-page version

| Step | Do this | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | List 10 to 20 customer questions | 1 hour |
| 2 | Rewrite page openings to answer first | A few hours |
| 3 | Add headings, FAQ and tables | A few hours |
| 4 | Check robots.txt, firewall and indexing | 1 hour |
| 5 | Complete listings and ask for reviews | Ongoing |
| 6 | Log answers monthly | 1 hour a month |
| 7 | Refresh quarterly | Half a day |

The times are our estimates for a small site.

## Questions, answered

### What is an AEO blueprint?
A step-by-step plan for making your content easy for search and AI systems to use as a direct answer. Ours has 7 steps, from picking questions to refreshing pages.

### Is AEO different from SEO?
Mostly overlapping. SEO aims to rank pages and AEO aims to be the answer. Google says optimizing for its AI features is still SEO.

### How long does AEO take?
No reliable timeline is published. See [how long GEO takes](/blog/how-long-does-geo-take).

### Do I need a tool for AEO?
No. A spreadsheet, Search Console and your own pages are enough to start.

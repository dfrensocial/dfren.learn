# Which websites do AI assistants cite most? What studies show and don't

Wikipedia and Reddit appear at or near the top in most public studies of what ChatGPT cites, and Reddit and YouTube show up strongly for Google's AI Overviews and Perplexity. But the exact shares differ a lot between studies because they count different things. None of them says much about a small local business.

Every study below comes from a company that sells AI or SEO tools. We list the method and the limit for each.

## What the studies found

| Study | What it found | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns), 680 million citations, Aug 2024 to Jun 2025 | ChatGPT: Wikipedia was 7.8% of all citations. Reddit was 6.6% of Perplexity's citations and 2.2% of Google AI Overviews'. | Vendor. The widely quoted "47.9%" for Wikipedia is its share of ChatGPT's top 10 sources only, not of all citations |
| [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/most-cited-domains-llms/), US, Jan to Feb 2026 | ChatGPT browsing: Wikipedia 13.15%, Reddit 11.97% of citations | US only, and the page's totals are inconsistent |
| [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-in-chatgpt/), US, monthly updated | Wikipedia, Reddit, Consumer Reports, Walmart and PubMed lead ChatGPT's mention share | Share of the top 50 sources only. Sample size not disclosed |
| [BrightLocal, 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/), about 200,000 local searches | Across ChatGPT, AI Mode and AI Overviews, Google Business Profile was 28.63% of citations and Yelp 9.53%. Business websites made up 42% | US locations only |

The first three are about broad, general questions. The BrightLocal data is about local questions, and it looks quite different: business websites and listings matter far more than Wikipedia.

## Why the numbers don't match

- **Different denominators.** Some studies count share of all citations. Others count share of the top 10 or top 50 sources.
- **Different assistants.** ChatGPT leans on Wikipedia. Perplexity and AI Overviews lean on Reddit and YouTube.
- **Different questions.** General questions pull in encyclopaedias. Local ones pull in business pages and listings.
- **Different dates.** These tools change often. Ahrefs updates its page monthly.

A BrightEdge analysis reported by [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/comparison-of-ai-citation-patterns-offers-strategic-seo-insights/573327/) found that sources cited by different engines overlapped only 16% to 59% of the time. So "what AI cites" depends on which AI you mean.

## What does it mean for a small business?

This is our reading, not a rule.

1. **You are unlikely to be Wikipedia, and you don't need to be.** For local and buying questions, business websites and listings are a large share in the BrightLocal data.
2. **Be present where your buyers' questions get answered.** Review sites, directories and, in some categories, Reddit and YouTube.
3. **Make your own site citable.** Facts, prices, sources and a clear answer in the first lines.
4. **Don't chase one list.** Pick the assistants your customers use and check them directly. Here's [a free method](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business).

## Questions, answered

### Does ChatGPT mostly cite Wikipedia?
In several studies Wikipedia is the top single source for ChatGPT, but its share of all citations is modest in Profound's data (7.8%). Check which denominator a number uses before quoting it.

### Is Reddit important for AI visibility?
It's a large cited source in Profound's data for Perplexity (6.6%) and AI Overviews (2.2%). Whether it helps your business depends on whether your buyers discuss your category there. Be honest and disclose affiliation if you post.

### Do AI assistants cite small business websites?
Yes. In BrightLocal's local study, business websites were 42% of citations. That's US data from a company that sells local SEO software.

### Which AI cites the most diverse sources?
We found no clean comparison. Search Engine Journal's report on BrightEdge says the top 10 sources made up 18.5% of ChatGPT's and 26.7% of Perplexity's citations, but its figures are inconsistent in places, so we wouldn't rely on them.

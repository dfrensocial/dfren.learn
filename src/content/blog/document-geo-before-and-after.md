# Before-and-after proof: how to document that GEO worked (honestly)

To document GEO results, record a dated baseline, make a defined set of changes, re-run the same checks at about three months and report what moved, with the method. Show appearance rate across repeated runs and traffic or impression data, and say you can't prove the changes caused it. A real before-and-after from one client is what helps win the next one, as long as it stays honest.

We teach this as the last step of our course, so we have a stake. The rules below also protect you under India's advertising rules.

## What do you need to record at the start?

Everything in our [baseline checklist](/blog/what-to-track-before-geo-client): traffic, Search Console queries, AI impressions, a dated prompt panel, crawler access and page state. Dates matter most. A baseline with no date is hard to defend.

## What do you record at the end?

The same items, in the same way, about three months later. Keep prompts identical, run them the same number of times and note the assistants and settings. Add a list of every change you made and when.

## How do you report AI answer results?

Don't show one screenshot. AI answers vary: in one study ChatGPT and Google's AI returned the same brand list [less than 1% of the time](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/), and that study found how often a brand appeared across many runs was steadier than its position. So report:

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Runs where the client was named, per prompt | e.g. 2 of 9 | e.g. 5 of 9 |
| Appearance rate across the panel | Calculated | Calculated |
| Competitors named most often | Listed | Listed |

The numbers in the example are placeholders for the layout. Use your own data. A rise is a hint, not proof.

## What other evidence helps?

- **Search Console.** Impressions and clicks, and Google's [Generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) for AI Overviews and AI Mode impressions.
- **Analytics.** Visits from AI assistants. They're small: AI chatbots sent [0.28% of web traffic](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/) in Ahrefs' March 2026 tracker, so keep expectations modest. See [how to see AI referrals in GA4](/blog/see-if-chatgpt-sends-visitors-to-your-site).
- **Business outcomes** the client already tracks, such as calls or enquiries, only if they have a source.

## What can't you claim?

- **That GEO caused the change.** Many things move at once: seasons, ads, competitors and the assistants themselves.
- **A typical result.** One client isn't typical. Say it's one case.
- **Guaranteed results for the next client.**

India's [CCPA guidelines](https://ccpa.doca.gov.in/files/CCPA_Notification.pdf) require claims about facts to be capable of substantiation, say an endorsement must reflect the endorser's genuine opinion and require disclosure of any connection that might affect credibility. The [ASCI Code](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code) requires truthful claims and, where research is cited, its source and date. They don't name GEO, so apply the general test. This isn't legal advice.

## A case study structure

1. **Client and situation:** type of business, what they wanted (with their permission).
2. **Baseline:** date, method, numbers.
3. **What we changed:** a dated list.
4. **What we measured at three months:** same method, numbers.
5. **What it does and doesn't show:** caveats, in plain words.
6. **Client comment,** with their name and role, only if they agree and you disclose any relationship.

## Questions, answered

### How do I prove GEO worked for a client?
You can't prove cause. You can show a dated baseline, a list of changes and the same measures three months later, and report what moved with caveats.

### Can I use a client's results in marketing?
With their permission, using real dates and methods, and not implying typical results. Disclose any connection.

### What if the numbers didn't move?
Report that honestly and review what to change. A clear record is still useful, and trust matters more than a good-looking chart.

### How many prompts and runs should the panel use?
We suggest 10 to 20 prompts, three runs each, as a practical start. That's our rule of thumb, not a researched number.

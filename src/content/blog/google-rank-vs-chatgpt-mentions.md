# Why ranking #1 on Google doesn't mean ChatGPT will mention you

Ranking well on Google helps, but it doesn't decide whether an AI assistant names you. In one [Ahrefs study of 15,000 queries](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/), only about 12% of the links cited by ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot were in Google's top 10 for the same question. For Perplexity it was 28.6%. Other studies found higher numbers, so the honest answer is: it depends on the assistant.

We sell a GEO course, so we'd like you to care about this gap. The studies below are all from companies with their own products, and we've listed their limits.

## What the studies found

| Study | Finding | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Ahrefs, Aug 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/) | 15,000 long-tail queries. ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot: about 12% of cited links were in Google's top 10. Perplexity: 28.6%. | Data from early July 2025. Ahrefs sells SEO tools. |
| [Semrush, Jul 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-mode-comparison-study/) | 5,000 keywords. Perplexity cited domains in Google's top 10 over 91% of the time. AI Overviews about 86%. AI Mode about 54%. | Desktop only, one snapshot. Measures domains, not pages. |
| [Ahrefs, Mar 2026, via Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overview-citations-from-top-ranking-pages-drop-sharply/568637/) | Share of AI Overview citations from Google's top 10 fell from 76% to 38%. | The method changed, so the two numbers aren't directly comparable. |
| [BrightEdge, Sep 2025](https://www.brightedge.com/resources/weekly-ai-search-insights/rank-overlap-after-16-months-of-aio) | "Only 16.7% of citations come from top 10 results" for AI Overviews. | Sample size not stated. |
| [Seer Interactive, Feb 2025](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results) | Over 87% of ChatGPT search citations matched Bing's top 20. Only 56% matched Google's. | About 100 questions, most with "best" or "top". |
| [Seer Interactive, Jan 2025](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/what-drives-brand-mentions-in-ai-answers) | Brand mentions had a correlation of about 0.65 with page-one Google rank. | GPT-4o through the API, 10,000 questions, finance and SaaS only. |

## Why they disagree

The studies measure different things, so the numbers can't be lined up in one list.

- **Pages versus domains.** A site can be cited from a page that ranks nowhere while its home page ranks well.
- **Citations versus mentions.** Seer's 0.65 is about brand names in the answer text, not linked sources.
- **Different assistants.** Perplexity looks closest to Google. ChatGPT looks closest to Bing, in Seer's data.
- **Different questions.** Long, specific questions behave differently from short ones.
- **Different dates.** Ahrefs' own AI Overview number moved from 76% to 38% in months.

Ahrefs suggests the gap may come from [query fan-out](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/), where the assistant runs several related searches and picks pages that rank for those, not for your original question. Ahrefs calls that a theory, and so do we.

## What Google itself says

Google's documentation says a page must be [indexed and eligible to be shown in Search with a snippet](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that there are no extra requirements. It doesn't say you need a top-10 rank. Being findable by Google is the entry ticket. It isn't a promise.

## What to do about it

This is our suggestion, based on the studies above.

1. **Keep your SEO basics.** Indexed, fast, clear pages still feed every assistant that searches the web.
2. **Don't assume rank equals visibility.** Check what each assistant actually says about you. See [how to check for free](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business).
3. **Cover the follow-up questions.** If an assistant splits a question into parts, a page that answers one part directly can be picked even if it doesn't rank first for the main phrase.
4. **Get mentioned elsewhere.** Reviews and articles that name you don't depend on your own rank.

## Questions, answered

### Is SEO still worth doing if AI assistants use different sources?
Yes. AI Overviews are built into Google Search, and Google says normal Search eligibility is the requirement. Assistants that search the web also read ordinary pages. SEO alone may not be enough, but dropping it removes your starting point.

### Which assistant is closest to Google's results?
In the studies above, Perplexity and AI Overviews overlapped most with Google's top 10. ChatGPT overlapped least with Google and more with Bing in Seer's data. Check each assistant separately rather than assuming.

### Can I be cited without ranking at all?
Yes. In the Ahrefs data, roughly 80% of the URLs cited by ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot [did not rank anywhere in Google](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/) for the original query. Being cited doesn't require a top-10 position, though ranking helps.

### Should I trust these numbers?
Treat them as direction, not fact. Every study here comes from a company that sells tools, samples are limited and the assistants change often. Re-check in a few months.

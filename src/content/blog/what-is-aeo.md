# What is answer engine optimization (AEO)? How it differs from GEO and SEO

Answer engine optimization (AEO) means writing and structuring your content so search engines and AI tools can lift it out as a direct answer to a question. It covers featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, voice assistants and AI answers. There's no official standard definition, and different agencies use the word in slightly different ways.

We looked at what guides say and at Google's own pages. We found no standards body that defines AEO, so treat any single definition, including ours, as a working one.

## What does AEO include?

Guides that explain AEO usually list the same surfaces: featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice assistants, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and chat assistants. The shared idea is a question in, one short answer out. The usual tactics are to lead with a concise answer, write FAQ and how-to content, and use clear headings.

Many of these guides are written by agencies and software vendors, so tactics like "write 40 to 60 words" are habits that people use, not rules published by a platform.

## How is AEO different from GEO?

The terms overlap, and people use them loosely. A common split is:

| | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Direct-answer surfaces, where one source wins | Generated answers that combine several sources |
| Examples | Featured snippet, voice reply | ChatGPT answer, AI Overview |
| Typical goal | Be the source of the answer | Be named or cited inside the answer |

Google's own AI guide notes that AEO and GEO are [terms you may see used](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide). It says optimizing for generative AI search is still SEO, and it says to weigh third-party GEO and AEO advice against Google's guidance. Our [GEO vs SEO vs AEO guide](/blog/geo-vs-seo-vs-aeo) explains how we use each term, and [what is GEO](/blog/what-is-geo-in-plain-words) covers the other half.

In a [Google Trends chart for India](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?q=AI%20SEO,AEO,generative%20engine%20optimization,GEO%20course,chatgpt%20seo&geo=IN&date=today%205-y) over five years, "AEO" scored about 28.5 over the last 52 weeks, against about 2.2 for "generative engine optimization" in the same chart. Trends scores are relative, from 0 to 100, not search volumes. It suggests people in India meet the word AEO more than GEO for now. The word "geo" also means geography, which can muddy GEO searches.

## What should you do about AEO?

1. **Put the answer first.** Open each page and section with the direct answer, then explain.
2. **Write real questions as headings,** and answer them directly. Our FAQs below are an example.
3. **Keep the page crawlable,** since Google says a page must be [indexed and eligible to show with a snippet](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) to appear in its AI features.
4. **Use structured data only if it matches the page.** Google says it isn't required for generative AI search. See [schema markup for AI answers](/blog/schema-markup-for-ai-answers).

## Questions, answered

### What does AEO stand for?
Answer engine optimization. An "answer engine" is any system that gives a direct answer, from a Google featured snippet to a chatbot.

### Is AEO the same as SEO?
No, but they share most work. SEO aims to rank pages. AEO aims to get your content used as the answer. Google's guide says optimizing for its AI search is still SEO.

### Is AEO the same as GEO?
They overlap and many people use the words interchangeably. We use AEO for direct-answer surfaces and GEO for generated answers that cite several sources.

### Do I need AEO for voice search?
Voice assistants usually read one short answer, so a clear, direct answer near the top of the page helps. We found no official figure on how voice assistants choose sources.

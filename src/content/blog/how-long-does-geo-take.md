# How long does GEO take to show results? What is known and what isn't

Nobody has published a reliable timeline for GEO results. What is documented are small delays: OpenAI says a robots.txt change can take about 24 hours to take effect, and Google says a requested crawl can take a few days to a few weeks. Content then has to be crawled, indexed and chosen for an answer, so we tell clients to expect weeks to a few months and to review at about three months. That range is our experience, not a measured result.

We sell GEO training, so we'd like results to be quick. We can't honestly say they are.

## What delays are documented?

| Step | What the source says |
|---|---|
| Crawler setting change | OpenAI says it can take [about 24 hours](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) for its systems to adjust after a robots.txt update. Perplexity says [up to 24 hours](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots) |
| Google crawling a new or changed page | Google says crawling can take [anywhere from a few days to a few weeks](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl) and requesting it doesn't guarantee inclusion |
| Being picked for an AI answer | No official timeline. OpenAI says [placement is not guaranteed](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt) |

Notice the gap in the last row. Even once a page is crawled and indexed, whether and when an assistant uses it is not published by any company we checked.

## Why can't anyone give a number?

- **Answers vary run to run,** so "I appeared once" isn't a result. See [why answers differ](/blog/why-chatgpt-answers-differ-every-time).
- **Assistants change often.** Models and search features are updated, and a study from last quarter may not describe this one.
- **Many things change at once** on a real site, so it's hard to say which fix caused a gain.
- **We found no study of the time from a content change to a citation.** If you find one, check its sample and dates.

## What should you tell a client or yourself?

This is our practice, from our course material:

1. **Say on day one that it takes time.** Content must be published, crawled, indexed and then actually cited.
2. **Plan for weeks to a few months, not days.**
3. **Check again at about three months,** against a baseline taken before work began.
4. **Report appearance rate, not position,** because position in one list is noisy.

## How do you measure progress?

- **Before you start:** save a [baseline](/blog/what-to-track-before-geo-client) of traffic, Search Console queries and a panel of AI answers.
- **Monthly:** repeat the same prompts and log how often you're named. Our [free method](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business) shows the sheet.
- **Traffic:** look at [AI referral sources in Google Analytics](/blog/see-if-chatgpt-sends-visitors-to-your-site). Remember AI chatbots sent [0.28% of web traffic](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/) in Ahrefs' March 2026 tracker, so small numbers are normal.
- **Google's side:** Search Console has [Generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) showing impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode.

## What can slow results down?

Blocked crawlers, unindexed pages, thin pages with no facts, and no mentions on other sites. Fixing the basics first is the fastest honest route. See the [GEO checklist](/blog/geo-checklist-2027).

## Questions, answered

### How long does GEO take?
There's no published timeline. Technical delays are hours to weeks. Beyond that, we suggest planning for weeks to a few months and reviewing at about three months.

### Can I see GEO results in a week?
Possibly for technical changes, such as a crawler setting taking effect in about a day. Seeing your business named more often in assistants within a week is unlikely, and we found no evidence of it.

### Is GEO faster than SEO?
We found no data comparing them. Both depend on crawling and indexing, and Google says optimizing for its AI features is still SEO.

### What should I do while I wait?
Keep your baseline, keep publishing useful pages, collect honest reviews and re-run your prompt panel monthly.

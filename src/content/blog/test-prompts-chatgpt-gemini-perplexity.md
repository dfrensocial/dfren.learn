# How to test your prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude

Write prompts the way your customers talk, keep them short, run each one in a fresh chat on every assistant and repeat each three times. Log the date, the assistant, whether web search was on and who was named. ChatGPT and Perplexity can be used without signing in, with limits. Gemini and Claude use a free account. Treat the result as a sample, not a ranking. This is a free quick win, not a reliable tracking method: AI doesn't give the same answer every time, so you may be cited on one run and not the next.

Our companion guide, [how to know if AI is recommending your business](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business), covers the spreadsheet. This one is about setting up the tests. We make an audit tool, so read our tool mentions with that in mind.

## Which assistants can you use for free?

|---|---|
| ChatGPT | OpenAI's help page says [people who aren't signed in can also use web search](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt) |

Limits and features change, so check each page. We didn't confirm Tamil support for Claude or Perplexity.

## How should you write the prompts?

A Semrush study of 115 prompts (June 2026) found short conversational prompts had a near-100% brand mention rate, while long structured prompts had about 2 to 3% in its data. It's a small sample from a tool vendor, but it points to a practical lesson: [test short, natural questions](https://www.semrush.com/blog/the-ghost-citations-study/), the way a customer would type them.

Mix four kinds:

1. **Category plus place:** "best physiotherapy clinic in Adyar, Chennai"
2. **Problem:** "who can fix a knee that hurts on stairs near T. Nagar"
3. **Price or comparison:** "how much does a root canal cost in Coimbatore"
4. **Your name:** "is [your business name] any good?"

Add the city or area, since OpenAI says ChatGPT uses your [approximate location](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt) and suggests asking with a neighbourhood or postal code.

## What should you keep the same each run?

- **Fresh chat for every prompt,** so earlier questions don't shape the answer.
- **The same wording,** when you want to compare over time.
- **Web search on or off,** noted each time. Claude's help page says in its newer experience there's [no toggle and it searches when it helps](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10684626-enabling-and-using-web-search), so note what you see.
- **Same device, location and logged-in state** where possible, since results can vary by place and account.

## How many runs?

SparkToro's study found the same brand list came back [less than 1 in 100 times](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/), and its researchers suggest 60 to 100 runs for a full picture. For a small business, three runs per prompt per assistant is a workable start. That's our rule of thumb, not a researched number. Ten prompts, four assistants and three runs is 120 answers, so start with two assistants if time is short.

## What do you record?

Date, assistant, prompt, run number, businesses named, whether you're named, whether your site is linked and the sources cited. Then calculate your appearance rate per prompt: runs where you're named divided by total runs.

## What if a prompt tests the language?

If your customers ask in Tamil, test in Tamil too. We found studies showing the prompt language can change which brands are named. See [does the language you ask in change the answer](/blog/does-prompt-language-change-ai-recommendations).

## Questions, answered

### Which assistant should I test first?
ChatGPT, since India has about [100 million weekly users](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/), then Gemini and Perplexity. Add Claude if you have time.

### Should I be logged in?
Either works, but stay consistent. Logged-in answers may use your history or settings.

### Do I need to use the same device?
It's safer, since location and settings can affect answers.

### How often should I repeat the test?
Monthly is enough for most small businesses. Keep the same prompts so numbers compare.

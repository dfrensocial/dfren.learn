# AI visibility tools by budget: what $29 to $499 a month buys (and ₹0 options)

AI visibility tools track whether assistants mention your brand across a set of prompts. Reported prices run from about $29 a month (about ₹2,747) to $489 or more for larger plans, and one big SEO suite sells its AI add-on at $99 a month per domain. Prices change, and most numbers below come from review sites and roundups, not the vendors' own pages. For a small business, a free spreadsheet is a fair place to start.

We make a different kind of tool (a site audit, not a prompt tracker), so we have an interest. We've marked each price as reported.

## What do these tools actually do?

Prompt trackers run your list of questions on a schedule across assistants and report how often your brand appears, with competitors and sources. They save the hours it takes to do this by hand. They can't see inside the assistants. Google's guide says [no third-party tool has access to its internal ranking or AI systems](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), so every tool measures outputs, the same way you do manually.

## What to ask before you pay

SparkToro's Rand Fishkin said any tool that gives a "ranking position in AI" is "full of baloney", according to the [study write-up](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/), and said visibility across many runs is a more reasonable metric. So ask:

1. How many times does the tool run each prompt? One run proves little.
2. Does it report appearance rate or only position?
3. Which assistants, and with web search on or off?
4. Can you see the actual answers and sources?
5. How are prompts chosen, and can you add your own, in Tamil too?

## Questions, answered

### Do I need a paid AI visibility tool?
Not to start. A spreadsheet works for 10 to 20 prompts. Paid tools help when you have many prompts or clients.

### What is the cheapest AI tracking tool?
HubSpot's AI Search Grader is free for a one-time check. Among paid trackers, Otterly's reported Lite plan at $29 a month is the lowest we found, with 15 prompts. Prices change, so verify.

### Are AI visibility tools accurate?
They measure what assistants say, and answers vary, so accuracy depends on how many runs they average. Ask the vendor.

### Do these tools improve my visibility?
No. They measure. Improvement comes from changes to your site and your presence elsewhere.

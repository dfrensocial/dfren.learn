# How much to charge for a GEO audit in India (in rupees)

Published GEO audit prices run from about ₹47,358 ($500) for a small site on a US consultant's list to ₹1,50,000 to ₹8,00,000 for an enterprise audit from an Indian agency. There's no independent price survey, and every source below sells GEO work. For a first audit on a small local site, price from your own hours, not from these ranges.

## Read these numbers with care

**Every source sells something.** Rioja runs consulting and courses, and his site lists a separate SEO plus GEO audit at $9,500. His article also says $500 to $1,000 in the body but shows higher tiers in its table. upGrowth is a growth agency, and its page doesn't say how it collected its ranges. Its audit range, ₹1,50,000 to ₹8,00,000, sits in enterprise territory.

**Scope drives the price.** upGrowth says audit cost depends on depth and the number of AI platforms you target. Rioja prices by site size.

**These aren't what clients paid.** They're list prices from sellers.

## What an audit contains

Rioja's [5-point check](https://alejandrorioja.com/geo-audit-pricing-what-to-charge-clients/) is a useful template. It covers:

1. Crawler access: whether AI bots can read the site (robots.txt and llms.txt)
2. Schema coverage and correctness
3. Entity clarity: whether the business is described the same way everywhere
4. Direct-answer structure on key pages
5. A baseline check of whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews mention the business

The deliverable is one findings document. Write yours the same way: what you checked, what you found, what to fix first, and nothing about guaranteed results.

## How to price your first audit

This is our view, not a published rule.

1. **List what you'll check.** Use the 5 points above.
2. **Estimate your hours.** For a small site, count reading pages, asking the AI assistants, and writing the document.
3. **Set an hourly rate you'd accept.** Multiply.
4. **Check it against what the client pays elsewhere.** One agency page says most small-business SEO retainers in India run [₹15,000 to ₹60,000 a month](https://seo.irpr.agency/pricing/small-business-seo/) (reported, agency marketing). A one-off audit that costs more than a month of their SEO will be a hard sell to a small shop.
5. **Say what you won't promise.** No rankings, no citations.

### An illustrative example

This is an illustrative example, not a real quote. You spend 8 hours on an audit of a 12-page clinic website. At ₹1,000 an hour, the price is ₹8,000. If you charged ₹8,000 and spent 12 hours, you'd earn about ₹667 an hour. Track your hours on the first one, so the second is priced better.

## Questions, answered

### How much does a GEO audit cost in India?
Published prices run from about ₹47,358 for a small site on a US consultant's list to ₹1,50,000 to ₹8,00,000 from an Indian agency. These are seller list prices, not surveys. Small local businesses rarely pay enterprise rates.

### How much should I charge for my first GEO audit?
Price from your hours. Estimate the time, set a rate you'd accept and add a little buffer. Compare with what the client pays for related work, such as monthly SEO. We can't give a figure, because no independent data exists.

### What does a GEO audit include?
A typical check covers crawler access, schema, how clearly the business is described, direct answers on key pages and whether AI assistants mention the business. Rioja's 5-point check is one public template. Deliver it as one findings document.

### Should I charge per site or per page?
The published sources price per site, scaled by size or number of locations. Rioja's lowest tier is for sites under about 50 pages. Start with a flat fee per site and state the page limit.

# GEO for small business: where to start on a zero budget (India)

You don't need a paid tool to start. Google says no third-party tool has access to its internal ranking or AI systems, and the useful first steps are free: check what AI says about you, complete your Google Business Profile, fix your top pages and watch Search Console. Do these seven in order. The whole list can take a weekend.

We sell a GEO course, but the steps below don't need it. We say at the end where it can help.

## Why bother

Customers have started asking assistants. India has about [100 million weekly ChatGPT users](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/), according to a February 2026 report. An Adobe survey reported by [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/65-of-indian-shoppers-trust-ai-recommendations-highest-in-asia-pacific-adobe-report-4061340) found around 65% of Indian respondents had used AI to search for personalised product recommendations. Adobe sells marketing software and the article doesn't give the sample size, so read it as a hint, not a measurement.

We found no India study on how often assistants recommend small local businesses. That's a gap, not a reason to wait.

## Step 1: Ask the assistants about your business (30 minutes)

Write 10 questions a customer would ask, such as "best dentist near Anna Nagar" or "who makes custom uniforms in Tiruppur". Ask each in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude, and record who is named. ChatGPT's help page says [people who aren't signed in can use web search](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt). Answers change between runs, so repeat the set a few times. The full method is in [how to check for free](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business).

## Step 2: Complete your Google Business Profile (1 hour)

Google India says [creating a Business Profile is free](https://business.google.com/in/business-profile/). Google's help page says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that businesses with [complete and accurate information are more likely to show up](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091). Fill in every field: name, address, hours, category, phone, website, photos and services.

Google's guide on AI features also says Business Profiles can help your products and services be visible in AI responses. It doesn't say assistants like ChatGPT read your profile, and we found no source that does.

## Step 3: Set up Search Console (30 minutes)

[Search Console](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9128668) is a free Google service. It shows which searches bring people to your site. Clicks from AI Overviews and AI Mode are [counted in the Web search data](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7042828), and Google added [Generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) in 2026 that show impressions in AI features. Take a screenshot now. It's your baseline.

## Step 4: Let the crawlers in (15 minutes)

Check your robots.txt file. If you use a firewall or CDN, make sure it isn't blocking OpenAI's search crawler. OpenAI's help page says to [allow OAI-SearchBot and OpenAI's published IP addresses](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt) to be eligible for ChatGPT search. See [how to get recommended by ChatGPT](/blog/get-recommended-by-chatgpt) for the details.

## Step 5: Fix your three most important pages (a weekend)

Pick your home page, your main service page and your contact page. For each:

- Put a plain answer in the first two lines: what you do, where, and for whom.
- Add facts with numbers: years in business, prices or ranges, areas served.
- Add a short FAQ with real customer questions, answered directly.
- Make your name, address and phone number identical everywhere.

Google says it wants [unique expert or experienced takes](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) that go beyond common knowledge. Your own experience, local detail and real prices are exactly that.

## Step 6: Get listed and reviewed (ongoing, free)

AI answers draw on more than your own site. In an [SE Ranking study](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/new-data-top-factors-influencing-chatgpt-citations/561954/), sites listed on several review platforms averaged more ChatGPT citations than those that weren't. It's a correlation across large sites. Still, asking happy customers for honest reviews and being listed in the directories your trade uses costs you nothing but time.

## Step 7: Refresh and re-check every quarter

Update your three pages with new facts every three months and repeat Step 1. Compare with your first record. Our research on [how long GEO takes](/blog/learn-geo-30-days) is thin, and most advice says weeks to months, so judge at about the three month mark.

## What to skip at the start

- **Paid AI visibility dashboards.** They can help you track later. They don't do the work.
- **Special files.** Google's guide says you don't need new machine readable files or llms.txt to appear in Google Search.
- **Rewriting everything.** Three good pages beat thirty thin ones.

## Questions, answered

### Can a small business do GEO without hiring an agency?
Yes, for the basics. Steps 1 to 7 need no agency or tool. An agency can help with writing, technical fixes and tracking. Published agency prices in India start around ₹35,000 a month, so try the free steps first.

### Do I need a website for GEO?
Mostly yes. Studies of local ChatGPT searches found business websites were the largest source type, at [58% in a 2024 BrightLocal study](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/uncovering-chatgpt-search-sources/) of US searches. If you have no website, start with a complete Google Business Profile and listings.

### How much does this cost?
The steps cost nothing but time. Domain, hosting and any paid listings are your normal costs. We don't recommend paying for guaranteed AI mentions.

### Should I write in Tamil or English?
Write in the language your customers use. Tamil is [supported in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8357869-how-to-change-your-language-setting-in-chatgpt), and the Gemini app lists it too. Test your own Tamil and English questions and see which version gets you named.

# Free GEO audit tools compared: what each one actually checks

Some GEO audit tools check your website, and others ask AI models what they say about your brand. Juma's GEO audit is free and needs no sign-up. HubSpot's AI Search Grader is a free one-time check of how three assistants describe a brand. Litmus, our own tool, is rule-based and charges per scan, starting at ₹49. They measure different things, so their scores aren't comparable.

We make Litmus, so we have an obvious stake. We opened each tool's own page and report only what it says. We didn't run the tools on a client site for this article.

## What does each type tell you?

**Site audits (Juma, Litmus)** check whether your pages are readable, answer-first, fresh and well structured. They tell you what to fix. They can't tell you whether an assistant names you today.

**Brand perception checks (HubSpot's grader)** ask models what they say about you. They tell you whether the assistant knows you. HubSpot says the result is based on training data, so a young or small business may score low whatever its website looks like.

**Manual prompt checks** show what real answers look like on a given day, but vary run to run. See [why ChatGPT gives a different answer every time](/blog/why-chatgpt-answers-differ-every-time).

You'll learn the most by using one of each: a site audit, a perception check and a manual panel.

## How do you pick?

- **Zero budget, first look:** start with Juma or HubSpot's grader and the manual method.
- **You'll audit more than a couple of sites, or want a repeatable score you can show a client:** a rule-based tool such as Litmus, so the same site gets the same score each time. That's our design goal, and you can test it by scanning one site twice.
- **You want to track mentions over months:** the manual sheet in [our free method](/blog/know-if-ai-recommends-your-business) works. Paid trackers exist, and we compare prices in [AI visibility tools by budget](/blog/ai-visibility-tools-by-budget).

## What to be careful about

- **Scores from different tools can't be compared.** They count different things.
- **Prompt-based tools vary.** A tool that wraps your URL in a chatbot prompt inherits the chatbot's run-to-run variation. Ask any vendor how they keep scores repeatable.
- **No tool sees Google's systems.** Google's guide says no third-party tool has access to its internal ranking or AI systems.
- **A score isn't a result.** It's a to-do list.

## Questions, answered

### Is there a completely free GEO audit tool?
Juma's page says its audit is free with no sign-up. HubSpot's AI Search Grader is also free for a one-time check. Both pages are the vendors' own claims.

### Do GEO audit tools give accurate scores?
They give consistent scores only if the method is rule-based. A tool that sends your URL to a chatbot can return a different score each run. Test any tool by scanning the same site twice.

### Which tool is best for small business?
For a first look, a free site audit plus the manual method. In our view, a repeatable tool becomes useful once you have several sites to compare.

### Can I just ask ChatGPT to audit my site?
You can, but it has no fixed rubric. See [why that gives a different score every time](/blog/why-chatgpt-gives-different-website-score).

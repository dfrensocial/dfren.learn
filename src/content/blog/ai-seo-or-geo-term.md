# AI SEO or GEO? Which word to use on your service page (India search data)

Use the word your customer types. In India, that's "AI SEO" or "AEO", not "GEO". Over the last 52 weeks, Google Trends scored AI SEO at 28.3 and AEO at 28.5, against 2.2 for "generative engine optimization". So AI SEO and AEO were searched about 13 times more. Put GEO in brackets and explain it.

These are relative scores, not search volumes. Here's how we read them, with the limits.

## The numbers

We pulled a [5-year Google Trends chart for India](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?q=AI%20SEO,AEO,generative%20engine%20optimization,GEO%20course,chatgpt%20seo&geo=IN&date=today%205-y) on 3 November 2026. All 5 terms sit in one chart, so the scores are comparable. We read the values off the chart, so they're accurate to about 1 point.

| Term | Last 52 weeks | The 52 weeks before | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI SEO | 28.3 | 17.2 | Up about 65% |
| AEO | 28.5 | 16.2 | Up about 76% |
| ChatGPT SEO | 5.4 | 6.4 | Down about 16% |
| generative engine optimization | 2.2 | 0.6 | About 3.7 times, from a tiny base |
| GEO course | 0.7 | 0.1 | Too small to trust |

Google Trends scores run from 0 to 100, and 100 is the busiest point in that chart. A term scoring 28 isn't searched 28 times. It's searched about 13 times as often as a term scoring 2.2.

## What the numbers do and don't say

**AI SEO and AEO are both rising.** Both grew by about two thirds or more over the year.

**"ChatGPT SEO" is flat to falling.** It peaked at about 10 in early December 2025 and has slipped since. If you wrote a page around that phrase, it's the weakest of the three.

**The GEO term is small.** It grew fast, from 0.6 to 2.2, but it's still about 13 times smaller than AI SEO. Small numbers move a lot, so don't read too much into the growth.

**A spike isn't demand.** AEO and AI SEO both peaked in the week of 2 August 2026, with AEO at 100. In that period, AEO's top rising searches were about a named person, so part of the spike is a personality, not interest in the topic.

**"GEO" is polluted.** Google's own [suggestions for "geo course"](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&q=geo%20course&hl=en&gl=in) include other subjects, such as geo engineering courses. So even the small "GEO course" number includes people who want a different subject.

## What to put on your page

1. **Lead with the word customers type.** For a service aimed at business owners, say "AI SEO" or "get found in ChatGPT".
2. **Put GEO in brackets once.** "AI SEO (also called GEO, or generative engine optimization)" tells both audiences and search engines what you mean.
3. **Use AEO if you also talk about featured answers.** It has the same weight as AI SEO.
4. **Don't quote the scores as volumes.** Say "searched about 13 times more", not "searched 13,000 times".

## An illustrative example

This is an illustrative example, not a real client. A Chennai interior design studio wants to be named when someone asks ChatGPT for a designer in Adyar.

- **Weak heading:** "GEO services in Chennai". Few people search this.
- **Better heading:** "AI SEO for Chennai interior designers: get named when people ask ChatGPT".
- **First paragraph:** "AI SEO, also called GEO, is the work of making your studio easy for AI assistants to find, understand and recommend." That covers 3 terms in 1 sentence.

## Questions, answered

### Is GEO the same as AI SEO?
Mostly, yes. Both describe making your content easy for AI assistants to find and cite. "GEO" is the term from the 2023 research paper, while "AI SEO" is the everyday label. Read [GEO vs SEO vs AEO vs AI SEO](/blog/geo-vs-seo-vs-aeo) for the differences.

### Which term do Indian customers search for?
AI SEO and AEO, about equally. Over the last 52 weeks they scored 28.3 and 28.5 on Google Trends in India, against 2.2 for "generative engine optimization". These are relative scores in one chart, not volumes.

### Should I stop using the word GEO?
No. Use it where practitioners read, like proposals, courses and technical pages. On pages meant for customers, lead with AI SEO and put GEO in brackets. Different readers use different words.

### Do these numbers show how many people search?
No. Google Trends gives a 0 to 100 index relative to the busiest point in a chart. For real monthly volumes you need an export from Google Keyword Planner or a similar tool. We haven't used one for this article.
